# 00.06 · Third-party content — Larian, Wizards of the Coast, SRD

*Consulted 2026-10-11 from the official pages. Policies change — re-check before every public release and
update the dates. Not legal advice.*

## Larian — Baldur's Gate 3 Fan Content Terms
Source: <https://baldursgate3.game/modding-terms> — **last updated 31 July 2025**, read 2026-10-11.
- BG3 fan content follows these terms, not Larian's general fan-content policy.
- The Toolkit licence is limited and revocable, only for making mods for the game.
- Sharing publicly grants Larian and its partners an irrevocable, worldwide, royalty-free licence to use
  the content (including through the in-game Modding Library run by mod.io).
- **No compensation** except as the Wizards Fan Content Policy allows (below).
- Content must comply with the **Wizards Fan Content Policy**, incorporated by reference.
- The author indemnifies Larian against third-party copyright / trademark claims and is solely
  responsible for the content.

## Wizards of the Coast — Fan Content Policy
Source: <https://company.wizards.com/en/legal/fancontentpolicy> — **last updated 15 Nov 2017**, read 2026-10-11.
- Fan content must be **free** — no paywall, survey, subscription or email gate. **Donations are allowed**
  (Patreon-style) as long as they don't limit access; ads and sponsorship allowed. → The mod's Ko-fi /
  PayPal support is compatible; never gate a download or early access behind them.
- **Required notice** on the mod pages (and in the mod's description):
  > Arcana Class is unofficial Fan Content permitted under the Fan Content Policy. Not approved/endorsed by
  > Wizards. Portions of the materials used are property of Wizards of the Coast. ©Wizards of the Coast LLC.
- No Wizards logos or trademarks (D&D logo, ampersand…) without written permission; don't strip notices
  from art that carries them.
- No verbatim copying of rules or books; no other people's IP without permission; no offensive content
  (the policy names racist, sexist, homophobic, transphobic, ableist, ageist content); no selling.

## SRD 5.1 (rules text)
Released under **CC BY 4.0**. If mod text reuses SRD wording, the attribution must say the work includes
material from the SRD 5.1 by Wizards of the Coast LLC, link the SRD, and state it is licensed under CC BY 4.0
with a link to the licence — and nothing else about Wizards. *Exact wording to copy from the SRD 5.1 PDF
front matter before use (not yet read directly).*

## What must never be redistributed
Assets or text extracted from the game (`31.04`, `56` — local only), Larian/Wizards logos, third-party
references in `41.02`.
