# 00.06 · Platform rules — mod.io and Nexus Mods

*Consulted 2026-10-11. Re-check before every public release.*

## mod.io (Larian's official pipeline, PC and console)
- Guidelines page: <https://mod.io/g/baldursgate3/r/modding-guidelines> — **could not be read automatically
  (JavaScript page). Owner to read it in a browser and summarise here.** ☐
- From Larian's announcements (secondary sources): mods go through stability and Terms-of-Service checks
  before appearing in the in-game browser; console availability needs an extra curation step; some
  script-heavy mods are not officially supported — Script-Extender mods stay outside the official
  pipeline. → **Console edition: no SE, through mod.io. PC edition with SE: Nexus.**

## Nexus Mods
Terms of Service: <https://help.nexusmods.com/article/18-terms-of-service> — **last updated 12 May 2026**,
read 2026-10-11.
- The author keeps ownership but grants Nexus and partners a broad, irrevocable licence (content may stay
  after deletion).
- Uploads must not infringe copyright or trademarks (including the game owner's) and must follow the game's
  EULA / terms; credit original authors when building on their work.
- The author sets the permissions other users get (modify / redistribute) at upload.
- Donation Points are discretionary; authors receiving rewards count as business users.
- **AI:** no mandatory disclosure in the ToS. Nexus has an **"AI-generated content" tag** that authors can
  apply and users can vote onto a page (news post "AI-Generated Content in Modding"). The ToS forbids using
  the site to train AI.
- Separate documents also apply: File Submission Guidelines and Forum / Commenting Guidelines. ☐ read
- Prohibited: illegal content, harassment, hate, piracy, malware; violent / graphic content must be tagged
  age-restricted.

## Studio rules derived from this
1. Every release page carries the Wizards notice (`THIRD_PARTY_CONTENT.md`).
2. Any asset made with AI is recorded in `ASSET_ORIGIN_REGISTER.md`; on Nexus apply the AI tag when the
   page contains AI-made assets (studio choice: disclose).
3. The SE requirement is stated on the PC (Nexus) page; the console edition never depends on it.
