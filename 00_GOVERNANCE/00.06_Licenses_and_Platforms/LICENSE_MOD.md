# 00.06 · Licence of the Arcana Class mod — PROPOSAL

*Status: proposed 2026-10-11 — the Owners choose. Constraints: the mod must stay free (Wizards FCP), Larian
and its partners already hold a licence to anything shared (BG3 Fan Content Terms), and Nexus asks the
author to set permissions at upload.*

## Proposed terms (to paste on the pages once approved)
- **Free to download and play.** Never sold; donations are welcome and never unlock content.
- **No re-uploads** of the mod or its assets to other sites without written permission from Lumox.
- **Compatibility patches and translations** are allowed if they require the original mod and credit
  Lumox; ask first for anything that redistributes Arcana files.
- **Assets** (icons, art, text) may not be reused in other mods without permission.
- Arcana Class is unofficial Fan Content — the Wizards notice from `THIRD_PARTY_CONTENT.md` applies.

## Alternative
A Creative Commons licence (e.g. CC BY-NC-SA 4.0) for text and art would let others build on the class
freely but non-commercially. Not recommended while the rework is in progress.

Decision → ADR when approved.
