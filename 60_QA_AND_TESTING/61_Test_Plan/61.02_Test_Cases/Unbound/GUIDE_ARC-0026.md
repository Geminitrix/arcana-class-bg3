## Probes

**Setup:** A fight with at least four enemies, spread out, and one ally in melee with an enemy. One character runs the probes.

### P1 · A Rush: no opportunity attack, paths around obstacles (SPEC_ARC-0026 P1 (the Rush carries her, WB5))

- **Steps:** Stand next to an enemy. Cast Probe: Rush on another enemy 6-9 m away, behind a table or a corner if you can
- **Expected:** The character runs to the target and strikes it; the enemy she leaves makes no opportunity attack; she goes around the obstacle
- **Fails if:** An opportunity attack on the way, or the Rush stops at the obstacle or refuses the target behind it

### P2 · Marked enemies can't be rushed (SPEC_ARC-0026 P2 (Rush only to an unmarked enemy))

- **Steps:** Cast Probe: Mark on one enemy. Open Probe: Rush and hover the marked enemy, then an unmarked one
- **Expected:** The marked enemy can't be targeted (greyed or refused); the unmarked one can
- **Fails if:** The marked enemy can be targeted

### P6 · A marked enemy makes no opportunity attack (SPEC_ARC-0026 P6 (the Open Path, WB10: the vanilla Mobile pair))

- **Steps:** Cast Probe: Open Path. Mark the enemy next to your melee ally with Probe: Mark. On the ally's turn, walk away from it. Then do the same next to an unmarked enemy
- **Expected:** Marked: no opportunity attack. Unmarked: the usual opportunity attack
- **Fails if:** The marked enemy still attacks (plan B: a mark that blocks every reaction)

### P7 · A hit marks; an ally's hit marks in her name (SPEC_ARC-0026 P7 (marks from hits — hers and an ally's))

- **Steps:** Hit an unmarked enemy with any attack (Probe Touch marks it). With Probe Open Path on you, let an ally hit another enemy
- **Expected:** Both enemies show Probe Windswept; the mark ticks down on your turns (2 of your turns)
- **Fails if:** No mark, the ally's hit does not mark, or the mark counts down on the enemy's turns

### P8 · The bag's X: exactly the marks counted (SPEC_ARC-0026 P8 (X counted; retest after 06_probe_count.patch))

- **Steps:** Mark 3 enemies (Probe: Mark or hits). Cast Probe: Count. Then mark 1 more and cast it again
- **Expected:** Probe Count appears on you showing 3 turns; after the second cast it shows 4. The number does not count down while you play (the goal sets it)
- **Fails if:** No status, no number on it, or the number is not the marks counted

### P3 · Turn start sweeps the marks; turn end is seen (SPEC_ARC-0026 P3 (the sweep at her turn start; her turn end))

- **Steps:** After P8, end your turn, then start your next turn
- **Expected:** At your turn end Probe Turn Ended shows on you; at your next turn start every Probe Windswept is gone
- **Fails if:** The marks stay, or Probe Turn Ended never shows

### P4 · The vortex pulls marked enemies from 9 m, the others from 6 m, once each (SPEC_ARC-0026 P4 (Where the Winds Meet, one area, WB49))

- **Steps:** Mark one enemy about 8 m from a point; leave another unmarked 5 m from it and a third unmarked 8 m from it. Throw Probe: Vortex at the point
- **Expected:** The marked enemy at 8 m and the unmarked one at 5 m are pulled to the point and Off Balance, once each; the unmarked one at 8 m is not

### P5 · Leap of Faith with the Force Tunnel animation (SPEC_ARC-0026 P5 (Leap of Faith on the Force Tunnel animation, WB7))

- **Expected:** A dash-through animation (the Force Tunnel tadpole power), striking each enemy in the path

### S2.1 · After an unarmed attack, Zephyr Rush carries you to an unmarked enemy (WB5, WB18 (Zephyr: Rush to the unmarked))

- **Steps:** Hit an enemy with an unarmed attack. Look at the bonus actions: cast Zephyr Rush on an unmarked enemy 6-9 m away, past another enemy

### S2.2 · A hit marks Windswept; a miss does not (WB8, WB49 (a hit marks, a miss does not))

- **Steps:** Hit one enemy with an unarmed attack or a card that deals damage. Miss another
- **Expected:** The hit enemy shows Windswept; the missed one does not
- **Fails if:** No mark on a hit, or a mark on a miss

### S2.3 · A failed save marks; Gale Burst has no free strike (WB8, WB28 (a failed save marks; Gale Burst loses its free strike))

- **Steps:** Cast Gale Burst on an enemy, then Wind Blast on a group
- **Expected:** Each enemy that fails the save is Windswept; one that succeeds is not. Gale Burst no longer gives a free unarmed strike
- **Fails if:** A successful save marks, a failed one does not, or Gale Burst still gives the free strike

### S2.4 · A Windswept enemy makes no opportunity attack (WB10, WB49 (the Open Path))

- **Steps:** Mark the enemy next to your melee ally. On the ally's turn, walk away from it. Then do the same next to an unmarked enemy
- **Expected:** Marked: no opportunity attack. Unmarked: the usual opportunity attack
- **Fails if:** The marked enemy attacks, or the unmarked one does not

### S2.5 · The mark lasts until the end of your next turn (WB9 (duration))

- **Steps:** Mark an enemy on your turn. End the turn, play the next one, end it
- **Expected:** Windswept counts down on your turns only and ends at the end of your next turn
- **Fails if:** It ends on the enemy's turn, after one of your turns, or stays longer

### S2.6 · Armour or a shield turns the Open Road off (WB3 (no armour or shield))

- **Expected:** No Windswept and no Zephyr Strike or Zephyr Rush. Take the armour off and they come back
