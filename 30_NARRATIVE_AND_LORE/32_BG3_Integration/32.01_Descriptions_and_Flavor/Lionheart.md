## The Weave in the features (W2)

- ***Maiden's Seal*** seals the beast's Weave — that is why it can't cast.
- ***The Garland I / II*** — the thread that binds the two forms.
- ***Infinity*** — the lemniscate's loop: both forms at once.
- ***The Twelfth Labour*** — the thread that doesn't end.

## Real source (S5)

**Stanley Rachman, *Fear and Courage* (1978):** courage is **approaching despite fear**, not the absence of
fear. It structures the rule: Courage comes from **approach** (ending the turn within the Villain's reach)
and from **threat** (being attacked by it) — never from killing.
