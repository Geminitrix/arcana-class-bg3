## 1. The class in one paragraph

The **Arcana** is a mortal chosen by **Mystra**, the Lady of Mysteries, and bound to the **Weave** by
her own hand. A seer first: the Arcana sees the threads of fate and pulls on them. Divination is the
base class's core (Portent, Expert Divination, Third Eye, Detect Supernatural) and it shows up as
out-of-combat power. Each subclass is a different destiny woven out of that seer, anchored in a Major
Arcanum of the tarot and awakening, in combat, into that card's **Aspect**.

**In-game class description (EN):**
> *"Bound to the Weave by Mystra's own hand, the Arcana are at once her chosen and her instruments.
> They draw upon mysteries granted to few, reshaping the flow of magic around them and turning it
> against those who would unravel it."*

**Tagline used on the mod pages:** *One class. Four destinies.*

### Pillars

| Pillar | What it means in play |
|---|---|
| **Mystra and the Weave** | Resources and cards are named for the Weave: you cast with **Woven Spell Slots**, you play cards with **Threads**, a lost card is **Unravelled**, a discarded one **Frayed**. |
| **Tarot** | Each subclass is a Major Arcanum; its capstone is that card's Aspect. The deck of cards itself is the tarot made mechanical. |
| **The seer** | The base class is utility and divination; the subclasses carry the combat. |
| **Destiny chosen at level 1** | The subclass is picked at character creation and defines the whole chassis (ability, saves, armour, weapons, sound set, outfit). |

## 2. Mystra and the Weave

- **Mystra** (Forgotten Realms): goddess of magic, keeper of the Weave — the fabric through which all
  arcane and divine magic flows. In Baldur's Gate 3 she is the patron behind Gale's story.
- The Arcana are **her chosen**: not wizards who study the Weave, but people the Weave was **woven
  into**. Their magic is not learned, it is granted and pulled.
- The base class is the **Aspect of Mystra**; each subclass is a different Aspect drawn from a tarot card.
- Weave vocabulary used in shipped text: *Threads, Woven, Weaving, Frayed, Unravel, Reweave, Tangle
  ("a knot in the Weave"), Bind a Thread, Spare Thread*. Mirrored Spell: *"Every spell woven near you
  leaves its reflection on Mystra's Weave."*
- 🎯 Design intent: a larger base-class rework will add spell selection and many more spells themed
  around Mystra and the Weave (see [07](07-open-questions.md) §1).

## 3. The tarot

Each subclass is anchored in one Major Arcanum. The card's number and name are written
`<number> - <Name>` in game (e.g. **0 - The Fool**). Fiction text avoids saying "tarot" outright.

| Subclass | Arcanum | Aspect of… | Tarot meaning that the subclass carries |
|---|---|---|---|
| 🍃 **The Unbound** | **0 · The Fool** | **Curiosity** | The beginning of the journey, freedom, the leap into the unknown, the traveller with nothing to hold them down. |
| 🪞 **The Deceiver** | **II · The High Priestess** | **Doubt** | Secrets and the veil between what is and what seems; the two pillars of truth and lie. Mystra is the Lady of Mysteries. |
| 🌟 **The Starchild** | **XVII · The Star** | **Hope** | Hope, healing and renewal after disaster; the light that guides. |
| 💀 **The Eternal** | **XIII · Death** | **Sorrow** | Endings and transformation — the one who crossed their own end and came back. |
| 🔴 **The Titan** *(in development)* | **VIII · Strength** | ❓ | Strength, courage, inner power and the taming of the beast. Characters and Aspect not defined yet. In the mod: ClassDescription, progression table (`7643bf5a`), icons in all 4 ClassIcons folders, selectable at level 1. |
| 🌈 **The Resonant** | **XIV · Temperance** | **Empathy** | Moderation, blending, balance and healing — pouring one cup into another without spilling. To temper is to bring two things into tune. |
| ⏳ **The Kingmaker** | **X · Wheel of Fortune** | **Anticipation** | The Rota Fortunae read literally: its four inscriptions (*Regnabo, Regno, Regnavi, Sum sine regno*) are the four positions; one rises as another falls. Built in the mod (progression `a688d11f`, formerly the Spinner placeholder). |

Aspect names in game: *0 - The Fool / 2 - The High Priestess / 17 - The Star / 13 - Death / 14 - Temperance / 10 - Wheel of Fortune* (Arabic numerals).

**Display names (ARC-0021, Owner, 2026-10-16):** the subclasses are shown in game as **The Windborn** (Unbound), **The Veilwalker** (Deceiver), **The Starchild**, **The Palerider** (Eternal), **The Lionheart** (was the Titan), **The Heartsong** (Resonant) and **The Kingmaker**. The names in the left column are internal: ids, folders and dossiers keep them (ADR-0002). The game text of the Unbound, the Eternal and the Resonant changes with their own build tickets.

## 5. Subclass identities (for writing anything new)

All subclass text is written in **one voice** (2026-10-07, `82_Automation/stats/subclass_texts.pl`):
- **Class description** (character creation): "Mystra wove the X from <what the Arcanum means>, a thread that <…>." + one sentence of what they do.
- **SUBCLASSES panel** (ProgressionDescription): "The X <verb> the Weave …", one sentence.
- **Tag line**: "We <archetype>, and the Weave <what it lets us do>." (model: the Resonant's).
- **Subclass name** (ARC-0021): one closed English compound — an image of the card or the theme + a word for the person (Star+child, King+maker, Lion+heart, Veil+walker, Wind+born, Pale+rider, Heart+song); no root another subclass already owns, except *heart*, shared by Lionheart and Heartsong by the Owner's choice.

| Subclass | One line | Tag line (in game) | Dialogue / sound set |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Arcana** (class tag) | Mystra's chosen. | *"We were chosen by the Lady of Mysteries, and the Weave answers when we pull on its threads."* | — |
| **Unbound** | Wind, open hands, open road. No armour, no fixed route. "Unbound" is freedom of movement, not rebellion — no friction with Mystra. | *"We leap before we look, and the Weave catches us wherever we land."* | Monk |
| **Deceiver** | The veil between what is and what seems: Doubles, Doubt, Seeds and binding lies (II · The High Priestess · Aspect of Doubt; names of DV29–DV31, in game with the ARC-0019 build). | *"We keep the veil between what is and what seems, and the Weave lets us stand on either side of it."* | Bard |
| **Starchild** | Starlight that restores. Healing, revival, solar and lunar wards. A healer, not an oracle — divination belongs to the base class. | *"We keep a light burning after every fall, and the Weave lets us pour it into the wounded."* | Druid |
| **Eternal** | Crossed their own end and came back. Endures what should kill them and commands the death of others. A juggernaut that wins by attrition. | *"We have crossed our own ending, and the Weave turns every wound we take into strength."* | Paladin |
| **Resonant** | Tempers one emotion with another (XIV · Temperance): Surges, moods and feelings on Plutchik's wheel. | *"We feel what you feel before you do, and the Weave lets us turn it into something else."* | Cleric |
| **Kingmaker** | Decides who reigns (X · Wheel of Fortune): allies and enemies ride one Wheel; one rises as another falls; a Court of offices named before battle. | *"We crown and we uncrown, and the Weave turns the wheel at our word."* | Sorcerer |

**In-game subclass descriptions (EN):**
- **The Unbound** — *"Mystra wove the Unbound from the first step of every journey, a thread that answers only to curiosity and the open road. They wear no armour and strike with open hands and moving air, slipping past every blow until the ground itself stops holding them down."*
- **The Deceiver** — *"Mystra wove the Deceiver from the veil between what is and what seems, a thread that no one watching can follow. They scatter Doubles that shatter when struck, sow Doubt until enemies can't trust their own eyes, and bind them with Sigils and chains."*
  *Game text today (Veil build). Draft for the ARC-0019 build (DV29), to pass the Narrative gate (`32.04`): "…They raise Doubles that splinter when struck, sow Doubt until enemies can't trust their own eyes, and bind them with Seeds and woven lies."*
- **The Starchild** — *"Mystra wove the Starchild from the light that remains after disaster, a thread of hope that never stops shining. They mend wounds that should have been fatal, shelter their allies beneath solar and lunar wards, and call the fallen back from the edge of death."*
- **The Eternal** — *"Mystra wove the Eternal from an ending they refused to accept, a thread that crossed death and came back changed. They take the blows meant for others and grow harder to kill for it, draining the life from their foes and calling the grave to finish what their hammer began."*
- **The Resonant** — *"Mystra wove the Resonant from the feelings that pass between every living thing, a thread that tempers one emotion with another. They surge joy, trust or fear into a creature's Weave, then braid what lingers into stronger feelings that lift allies and break enemies from within."*
- **The Kingmaker** — *"Mystra wove the Kingmaker from the crowns that rise and fall on Fortune's wheel, a thread that decides who reigns. They bind friend and foe to the turning wheel, crown their allies as their enemies hit the bottom, and turn the whole wheel over when fortune runs against them."*

## 7. Writing voice

- **Player-facing text is English**, in Larian's dry, compact register: the first sentence states origin
  or nature, the second gives the gameplay promise. Full rules: the `bg3-english-writing-style` memory
  (= `31.03_BG3_Style_Guide/ENGLISH_WRITING_STYLE.md`).
- Keep the **cards / deck** wording (tarot feel). Weave words for resources and piles (§2).
