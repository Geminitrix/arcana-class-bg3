# 31.02 · Theme — The Windborn (internal: Unbound) (0 · The Fool · Aspect of Curiosity)

**Fantasy:** *"Weightless, fast, and always exactly where you need to be."*
**Theme:** the wind on the open road — an unarmoured wanderer who touches every enemy once and is carried to the next,
then lets the gathered wind loose over the battle. Her question each turn is **Position**: where everyone is, and where
she goes next.

## From the card
A traveller steps toward a cliff edge looking up, a white rose in hand, a bundle on a staff, a small dog at the heels,
the sun behind; number **0**. Upright: beginnings, the leap, curiosity; reversed: recklessness (Waite, 1910). Atu 0 is
**Air** in the Thoth deck (Crowley, 1944) — checked in the primary text before any shipped text quotes them (DM-S7).

## From the myth and the real world
- **Aeolus** (Homer, *Odyssey* X) binds every wind in a bag and leaves only **Zephyrus** free to carry Odysseus home;
  the crew opens the bag out of curiosity. → **Aeolus' Bag** (the ultimate), **Zephyr Strike** (the free wind),
  **Where the Winds Meet** (every wind at once).
- **The Anemoi** (Hesiod, *Theogony*): **Boreas** the North Wind, **Notus** the South Wind, **Eurus** the East Wind.
- **The Beaufort scale**, force 0 (calm) to 12 (hurricane): **Breeze · Gale · Storm · Hurricane**, Zephyr's ranks.
- **Aang and the Air Nomads** (*Avatar: The Last Airbender*): the monk-like body, the glider, the deflection, a people
  who carry others.

## Ties to the lore bible
- **Mystra and the Weave:** *"Mystra wove the Windborn from the first step of every journey, a thread that answers only
  to curiosity and the open road."* (`31.01` §5; the class text still says *Unbound* until the build ticket.)
- **Unbound is freedom of movement, not rebellion** — no friction with Mystra (`31.01` §3).

## Vocabulary
Wind, road, step, touch, carry, gather, release, sweep, calm, gale, storm. Weave words where they fit: *a thread of
wind*, *the wind the Weave gathers*.

## What she is not
Not a rebel against Mystra; not a storm-calling caster (her wind is in her hands and feet); not a brawler who stands
and trades blows.
