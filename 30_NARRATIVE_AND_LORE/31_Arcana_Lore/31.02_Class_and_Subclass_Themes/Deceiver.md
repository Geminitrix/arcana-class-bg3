# 31.02 · Theme — The Veilwalker (internal: Deceiver) (II · The High Priestess · Aspect of Doubt)

**Fantasy:** *"No one watching can say which of her is real."*
**Theme:** the veil between what is and what seems — she fights with lies, and every lie an enemy believes plants
Doubt, until it can no longer trust its own eyes. Her question each turn is **Presence**: how many bodies she holds on
the field, and when she spends them.

## From the card
The High Priestess sits between two pillars, **B** and **J** — one black, one white — before a **veil embroidered with
pomegranates**. A scroll half hidden by her mantle holds a law she does not show; the moon lies at her feet (Waite,
1910; Pollack, 1980 — checked in the primary text before any shipped text quotes them, DM-S7).

## Ties to the lore bible
- **Mystra and the Weave:** *"Mystra, the Lady of Mysteries, keeps a veil between what is and what seems; the Deceiver
  was woven from it, a thread no one watching can follow."* Her Doubles are the Weave bent into her shape; *Binding
  Falsehood* binds with a woven lie.
- **Myth:** the **eidolon of Helen** (Euripides, *Helen*; Stesichorus' palinode) — the cloud-Helen for whom Greeks and
  Trojans fought while the real one was elsewhere. It names **Eidolon**, the card that summons Doubles. The Deceiver's
  myth alone: Ixion and Nephele belong to the Kingmaker (DV30).
- **History (real model):** the **Ghost Army** — the WWII deception unit of inflatable tanks and false radio, decoys
  that drew fire and made the enemy doubt what it saw.
- **Fiction:** **LeBlanc** (*League of Legends*) lends the kit's shape — a mark that detonates, a blink that leaves an
  image, binding chains, a recast, decoys — but **none of her ability names** (DV29). The **Bene Gesserit** (*Dune*):
  manipulation by word and voice (*Malicious Whispers*, *Marionette*, *Friends*).
- **Aspect:** Doubt — a point on Plutchik's wheel apart from the Resonant's emotions.

## Vocabulary
Use: **the Veil, veil, Double (Double of the Veil), lie, believe, Doubt, Deluded, Seed, burst, Splinter, reflect,
eidolon, pillar, mirror, whisper, face**.
Never: the LeBlanc names **Mirror Image, Sigil (of Malice), Distortion, Ethereal Chains, Mimic** (renamed DV29: Eidolon,
Seed of Doubt, Veilstep, Binding Falsehood, Twice-Told); **Shatter** for the Doubles' burst (it is **Splinter**; *Shatter*
is a vanilla spell); **clone** in player-facing text (the Doubles are illusions, not forms); **Empress** in any
player-facing text (internal ids only, DV12); **Nephele** (DV30).
She is not cruel for its own sake, not a fear-monger (fear is another subclass's), not a shapeshifter.
