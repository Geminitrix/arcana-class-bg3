# 31.02 · Theme — The Kingmaker (X · Wheel of Fortune · Aspect of Anticipation)

**Fantasy:** *"I don't win fights. I decide who does."* (SP96; mod-page tagline kept in reserve: *"Fortune is blind. I am not."*)
**Theme:** who rises, who falls, and when they cross — the Kingmaker never reigns; it crowns and uncrowns, and the
Wheel it turns is the same for friend and foe.

## From the card
The medieval **Rota Fortunae**: Fortuna turns a wheel with a figure at each point and an inscription for each —
*Regnabo* (I shall reign), *Regno* (I reign), *Regnavi* (I have reigned), *Sum sine regno* (I am without reign).
No one on the wheel controls it. The Kingmaker reads the card literally.

| Reading | Where the player meets it |
|---|---|
| The four inscriptions | The four positions Rising · Reigning · Falling · Fallen; each tooltip opens with its Latin |
| The wheel always turns | The Rota: every creature on the Wheel moves one position at the Kingmaker's turn |
| One rises as another falls | The Crossing: an enemy hits the bottom while an ally reigns |
| Whoever reigns will fall | Allies fall after they reign (until Tier III); enemies left on the Wheel reign again — and gain Hubris |
| The turning point | Peripeteia, the ultimate |

## Ties to the lore bible
- **Mystra and the Weave:** *"Mystra wove the Kingmaker from the crowns that rise and fall on Fortune's wheel, a
  thread that decides who reigns."* (SP95). The creatures on the Wheel are threads run through the Kingmaker's hands.
- **Myth:** Fortuna / Tyche (the Wheel, Tier I *Tyche*), Nemesis (Tier II, punishes hubris), Atropos (*Atropos' Cut*),
  Ixion (*Wheel of Ixion*).
- **Philosophy and history:** Boethius, "it is my nature to turn" (the core rule); Aristotle's *peripeteia*; *"Le roi est
  mort, vive le roi"* (*Succession*).
- **Science:** phase in a cycle — keeping the party and the enemies in anti-phase is the puzzle.
- **Fiction:** the Small Council of *A Song of Ice and Fire* (the Court); DIO, Faceless Void, Bard (*The Wheel Stops*).
- **Aspect:** Anticipation — every position says what comes next. The Resonant's *Weave Surge: Anticipation* is the same
  emotion for one turn; the overlap is deliberate.

## Vocabulary
Use: **the Wheel, on the Wheel, turn, position, rise, reign, fall, crown, uncrown, cast down, thread, cut, Crossing,
Hubris, King, Regent, Court, office, appoint**.
Never: **Spinner** (the retired working name), **Hand of the King** (it is *King's Hand*, SP101), the work-specific
Small Council titles replaced by SP101, **Cast Down / Crown / Nudge** as card names (renamed SP102).
Lowercase *wheel* only inside "Fortune's wheel"; the mechanic is always **the Wheel**.
