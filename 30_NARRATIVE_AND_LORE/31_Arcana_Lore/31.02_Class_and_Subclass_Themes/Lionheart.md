# 31.02 · Theme — The Lionheart (VIII · Strength · Aspect of Courage)

**Fantasy:** *"Every fight has a beast. You are the one who walks up to it."*
**Theme:** courage is fear faced, not fear absent — the Lionheart is paid only for approaching what is dangerous, and
fights the beast with two hands: the Maiden who tames it and the Lion who slays it.

## From the card
A woman closes a lion's jaws with bare hands, without effort; a garland binds them; the lemniscate floats over her
head. Strength took number VIII from Justice in the Rider–Waite deck.

| Reading | Where the player meets it |
|---|---|
| The woman and the lion | The two forms, the Maiden (open hands) and the Lion (steel) |
| Closing the jaw | The Maiden's sides close the beast's legs, footing, claws, jaw — and Strangle the Lion the whole beast |
| Courage as fear mastered | Courage, gained by standing in the Villain's reach |
| The garland | The Garland I / II |
| The lemniscate | The Aspect card Infinity; The Twelfth Labour, the thread that won't end |
| Justice's old number | The capstone Lion's Judgement |

## Ties to the lore bible
- **Mystra and the Weave:** *"Mystra wove the Lionheart from the moment fear is faced instead of fled, a thread looped
  back on itself that never comes to an end."* Maiden's Seal seals the beast's Weave.
- **Myth:** Heracles — the Nemean lion (Tier I, Strangle the Lion), the Hydra (Tier II), the twelfth Labour (Tier III).
- **Real source:** Stanley Rachman, *Fear and Courage* (1978) — courage is approaching despite fear.
- **Fiction:** Garen (Villain, Courage, Decisive Strike → Seal, Demacian Justice → Judgement), Jarvan IV (Pounce),
  Seiya and Naruto (The Twelfth Labour). **Practice:** aikido (Maiden's Throw).

## Vocabulary
Use: **beast, Villain, Courage, stand, reach, hand, Lion, Maiden, steel, sheathe, garland, Labour, hide**.
Never: **Titan** as a player-facing name (internal progression name only, LH1).
Card sides are written *Lion's X* / *Maiden's X*.
