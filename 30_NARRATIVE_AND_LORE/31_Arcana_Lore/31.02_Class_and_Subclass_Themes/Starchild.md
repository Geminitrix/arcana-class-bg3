# 31.02 · Theme — The Starchild (XVII · The Star · Aspect of Hope)

**Fantasy:** *"A healer whose magic is starlight given purpose."*
**Theme:** hope after disaster — the light that remains when everything has fallen. Each dawn she sings a Hymn to one
light, and when an ally sinks into the dark she brings the dawn back. Her question each turn is **Timing**: when to
heal, and whom. Hope shines brightest in the darkest hour.

## From the card
A figure kneels by a pool and pours water from two jugs, one onto the land and one into the pool, under one great
eight-pointed star and seven small ones. In the tarot sequence **The Star (XVII)** follows the Tower and is followed by
**The Moon (XVIII)** and **The Sun (XIX)** (Waite, 1910 — checked in the primary text before any shipped text quotes
it, DM-S7).

| Reading | Where the player meets it |
|---|---|
| Hope after disaster | **Dusk → Nightfall → Dawn**; the Awakening wakes only when the party is in danger |
| The figure pouring her jugs | **Outpouring**, the signature heal; **Elpis' Jar**, the ultimate; **Cleansing Rain** |
| The great star | **The Great Star**, the Aspect card |
| The light that guides | **Guiding Light** (Polaris); **Lantern** |
| Star, Moon and Sun in sequence | the three **Hymns** — of the Stars, of the Sun, of the Moon |

## Ties to the lore bible
- **Mystra and the Weave:** *"Mystra wove the Starchild from the light that remains after disaster, a thread of hope
  that never stops shining."* (`31.01` §5). **Tether** pulls an ally by a thread of the Weave.
- **Myth:** **Elpis**, Hope left in Pandora's jar when every evil had flown (Hesiod, *Works and Days*) — names the
  ultimate, and pairs with the Windborn's *Aeolus' Bag*. **Hesperus**, the evening star that rises at dusk, and
  **Eosphorus**, the dawn-bringer (Hesiod, *Theogony*) — name *Hesperus*, *Dawnbringer* and *Daystar*.
- **Ritual:** the **Orphic Hymns** to the Stars, to Helios and to Selene ground the three Hymns (to verify for DM-S7).
- **The real sky:** blue and golden hour, the sun dog, the moon ring, the nova, and the eclipse — *in an eclipse the
  stars come out by day*, the Tier III image.
- **Fiction:** **Soraka** (*League of Legends*) lends the shape — a healer who pays with her own life, a global
  answer — but **none of her ability names** (SC15). Lux lends nothing any more.
- **Aspect:** Hope — the moment after the worst, not optimism in easy times.

## The three lights
| Hymn | Verb | Feel | Skills it lights (Lantern) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hymn of the Stars | sharpen — all on one | the search | Perception, Investigation |
| Hymn of the Sun | spread — more targets, fire | the truth | Insight, Persuasion |
| Hymn of the Moon | linger — over time, cold | the lie, the hidden | Deception, Sleight of Hand |

## Vocabulary
Use: **Hymn, sing, Unsung, light, the Stars / the Sun / the Moon, Dusk, Nightfall, Dawn, answer, pour, tide, glow,
Afterglow, halo, star, eclipse, hope**. A heal *answers* a mark; it never *cures* or *cleanses* it.
Never: the Soraka and Lux names **Starcall, Astral Infusion, Rejuvenation, Equinox, Wish, Lucent Singularity, Final
Spark** (renamed SC15, SC32: Comet, Outpouring, Afterglow, Stillness, Elpis' Jar, Lantern; Final Spark leaves);
**path** for the daily choice (retired working name, SC1); **Prayer**, **Oath**, **Vow** or **Invoke** for a Hymn
(vanilla and deck words, SC3); **Dim Light** for Unsung (BG3's light system, SC5); **Moonlit / Sunlit** as Awakening
words (they leave, SC1).
She is not an oracle (divination is the base class's), not a damage caster, not a frontliner.

## Voice
Class line (`31.01` §5): *"…They mend wounds that should have been fatal, shelter their allies beneath solar and lunar
wards, and call the fallen back from the edge of death."* Tag line: *"We keep a light burning after every fall, and the
Weave lets us pour it into the wounded."* Both still fit; the Narrative gate may add the Hymns.
Unsung (SC5): *"Your light flickers, dim and waiting. Sing a Hymn to choose your light."*
