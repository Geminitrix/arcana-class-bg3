# The Crossing — passive

**Intent:** One rises as another falls.

**Rules in words:** A Fallen enemy on the Wheel takes Force damage once for each ally on the Wheel Reigning within 9 m — when the enemy enters Fallen and when an ally enters Reigning; once per (enemy, ally) pair per round; any cause counts. It moves nothing by itself (SP117).
