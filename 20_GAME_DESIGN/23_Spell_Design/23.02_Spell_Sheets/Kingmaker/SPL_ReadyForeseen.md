# Ready: Foreseen — Aspect card (created) + reaction

**Intent:** See the blow coming and turn the Wheel against it.

**Rules in words:** Readied until combat ends. When an enemy on the Wheel is about to attack you or an ally: Reigning → Falling (no 19–20 crit, −1d4), Falling → Fallen (Advantage against it, may cross). Rising or Fallen: nothing. It reacts once the attack is rolled: a Reigning attacker's −1d4 counts on that roll too (SP127).
