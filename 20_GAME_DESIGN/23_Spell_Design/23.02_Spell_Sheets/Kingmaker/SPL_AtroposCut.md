# Atropos' Cut — card (level 2)

**Intent:** Cut a thread off the Wheel where it hangs.

**Rules in words:** Enemy: removed from the Wheel, Slashing damage that grows the higher it stands (highest at Reigning, SP120). Ally: removed; if it was Reigning it keeps the Reigning effects until the end of its next turn; the King keeps the crown. Swift Shears (3): after Turn the Spoke it is free of action; Severance (4): after it the next Dethrone is free of action (SP123, SP124).
