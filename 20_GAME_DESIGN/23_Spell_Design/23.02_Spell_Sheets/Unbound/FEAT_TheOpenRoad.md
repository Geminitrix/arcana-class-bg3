# The Open Road — passive (core rule)

**Intent:** The wind marks every enemy she touches and carries her to the ones it hasn't touched yet

**Rules in words:** Any hit of yours or failed save against your effects makes an enemy Windswept until the end of your next turn; a Windswept enemy can't make opportunity attacks; after an unarmed attack or a card, Zephyr Strike as a bonus action
