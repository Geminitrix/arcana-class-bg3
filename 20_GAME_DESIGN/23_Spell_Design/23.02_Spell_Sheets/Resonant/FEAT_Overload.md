# Overload — passive

**Intent:** The Resonant feels everything they make others feel.

**Rules in words:** The meter fills +1 per peak, +2 per reaction; when a fight ends it settles to half (R53); waking Tier III fills it (R56). Full: Overloaded for turns equal to the character level — a flat penalty to Intelligence, Wisdom and Charisma saves and Psychic damage each turn (both by band 1/5/9), and once per turn a Surge that lands on an enemy deals it the same damage; no silence (R51, R52, R67). Emotional Overload transfers it.
