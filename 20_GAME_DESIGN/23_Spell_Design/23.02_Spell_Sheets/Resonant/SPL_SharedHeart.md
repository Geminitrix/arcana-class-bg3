# Shared Heart — card (level 5, capstone, Unique)

**Intent:** One feeling, felt by the whole party.

**Rules in words:** The Resonant and every ally within 18 m are linked until combat ends. A feeling braided in one linked creature is braided in all (counted once for refund, Overload, Awakening; R19, R46). A Catharsis on a linked ally heals every linked ally (R44). Feelings stack additively (R47).
