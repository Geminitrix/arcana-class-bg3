# Emotional Overload — created (ultimate)

**Intent:** Pour everything the Resonant felt into the enemy.

**Rules in words:** Wisdom save; every feeling on the target is spent either way. Fail: Psychic damage per feeling and Irresistible Dance 1 turn (2 at Tier III; charm-immune creatures don't dance). Success: half the damage, no dance. Either way: vanilla Mental Fatigue for turns equal to the Resonant's level. Ends Overloaded, empties the meter. Rank I one enemy within 18 m · II two enemies · III every enemy within 9 m of a point within 18 m (R51, R68).
