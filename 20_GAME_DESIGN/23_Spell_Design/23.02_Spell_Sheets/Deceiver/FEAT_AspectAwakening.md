# 2 - The High Priestess: Awakening (Veiled · Two Pillars · Aspect of Doubt) — passive + tiers

**Intent:** Apply Doubt to awaken the Aspect of Doubt for the rest of the fight.

**Rules in words:** Each stack of Doubt she or her Doubles apply counts 1; goals counted down on the progress status. Tier I Veiled: enemies with Doubt have Disadvantage on attacks against her and her Doubles, and The Veil Lifts. Tier II Two Pillars: once per turn, when an enemy becomes Deluded in her name, the nearest un-Seeded enemy nearby gains her Seed. Tier III Aspect of Doubt: the lie explodes (a Double destroyed by an enemy splinters by itself, Splinter's area grows) and Ready: Which One?; the old +3 Charisma and Charisma to damage leave (DV21).
