# The Veil — passive (visible, the core rule)

**Intent:** Every lie an enemy believes plants Doubt; at the third, it is Deluded.

**Rules in words:** Taught once on one visible passive, which also explains the Doubles (DV19). A lie believed: a Double destroyed by an enemy, an enemy caught in a Splinter, her Seed bursting on it, Malicious Whispers on a Seeded target. Doubles: 1 HP, fixed AC, their own player-controlled turn (movement and one action), immune to loss of control; +1 AC for her per Double; cap 3, 4 at level 7, 5 at level 12. Today the game teaches it on two passives (The Veil, Doubt).
