# The Hymns and Unsung — rule (statuses and card containers)

**Intent:** The Stars sharpen, the Sun spreads, the Moon lingers.

**Rules in words:** Every card is a container with three children, locked by the Hymn sung; same icon, the container on its neutral name, each child its own name. Stars, focused: the largest heal on one ally. Sun, radiant: a share spills to allies nearby and burns enemies next to the target; its heal on the main target stays well below the Stars' (SC33). Moon, tidal: part now, the rest over the next ‹2› turns; attackers are chilled and slowed. After a long rest she is Unsung until she sings: every child is locked; forgetting costs one bonus action.
