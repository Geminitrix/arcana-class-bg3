# Light in the Dark — passive

**Intent:** The light shines brightest when she is in the dark: the turn of her arc.

**Rules in words:** From level 10 she can fall into Dusk or Nightfall herself; her own mark is not consumed by heals, and while she carries it the heals she gives are ‹stronger›. Her bonus plus the target's mark never exceed Dawn. Outpouring's hit-point cost can push her into the dark. Replaces Stellar Anchor (SC26).
