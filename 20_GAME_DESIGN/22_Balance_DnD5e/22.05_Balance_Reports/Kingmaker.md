# 22.05 · Balance — The Kingmaker (current verdict)

The arithmetic of 2026-10-13, approved by the Owner with SP106–SP111, stays in `2026-10-13_ARC-0008_Kingmaker_Balance.md`.
This file is the current verdict after the later decisions SP112–SP114 (stage 6). Currency: `22.06/Arcana_Power_Budget.md`.
Built in the game (levels 1–12); **no probe and no balance playtest played yet**.

| Area | Verdict | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Whole kit at T2 | ✅ in band (≈ 92 %) | ≈ 2.2A per turn against 2.4A (report §6), mostly felt on the allies' turns |
| T1 (levels 1–4) | ✅ low on purpose (≈ 88 %) | No Court before level 5; the subclass "learns the Wheel" first |
| T3 before tiers and capstone | ✅ (≈ 83 %) | Tiers and The Wheel Stops close the gap |
| Crossing 1d4 / 1d6 / 1d8 Force | ✅ in band | ≈ 0.4A per turn; watch its share of the party's damage |
| Peripeteia (level-3 card, once per combat, SP108) | ✅ in band | As priced after SP108 |
| Master of Coin (once per Long Rest, SP109) | ✅ in band | Was 44 % of the budget before SP109 |
| **The Wheel Stops freezes the turn only (SP112)** | ✅ slightly stronger, still in band | The Kingmaker's cards keep moving creatures during the stop, so the capstone gains flexibility; the pressing damage is unchanged (report §8: alarm if it takes more than a quarter of a boss's HP alone) |
| **Ready: Foreseen readied with a Bonus Action (SP114)** | ✅ slightly weaker, in band | One Bonus Action once per combat; the reaction value is unchanged |
| **Two Kingmakers keep separate Wheels (SP113)** | n/a | No effect on a single Kingmaker's budget |
| Awakening 3 / 5 / 8 (a lone enemy counts 2) | ⚠️ watch | Report §8: Tier I later than turn 5 in a normal fight, or turn 4 against a lone boss, means too slow |

## What would prove it wrong (playtest, report §8)
Fewer than 2 Crossings a fight or the Crossing above 25 % of the party's damage · the players stop putting allies on the Wheel ·
Peripeteia decides every fight alone · The Wheel Stops takes more than a quarter of a boss's HP alone · Tier I after turn 5
(normal) or turn 4 (lone boss) · Master of Coin returns more than one slot a day.

**Owner's note (2026-10-13):** the Kingmaker's role sits close to the Resonant's (support through states); the class still lacks
a magical burst / DPR subclass — a future tarot card will fill it.
