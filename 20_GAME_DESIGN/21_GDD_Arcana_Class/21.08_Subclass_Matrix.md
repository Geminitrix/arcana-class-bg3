## What each one does that no other does

- **Unbound** — no armour, no weapons; highest speed and jump; turns every card into an extra attack.
- **Deceiver** — 1-HP Doubles to spend and Doubt that snowballs into Deluded; two-step damage (mark → detonate); copies other people's spells; disguises and telepathy.
- **Starchild** — healing, revival, cleansing; its Awakening converts healing into party damage.
- **Eternal** — heavy armour; spends its own HP as a resource; the only Aspect card that scales with damage taken.

## Level rhythm (when each subclass gets cards)

All four get cards at **1, 3, 5, 7, 9, 11** and their Awakening tiers at **3, 7, 12**. Levels 13–20
give the base class's capstone passives and, for Deceiver and Eternal, one extra card at 13
(Howl of the Dead). The Unbound keeps growing Twinkle Toes to 20.
