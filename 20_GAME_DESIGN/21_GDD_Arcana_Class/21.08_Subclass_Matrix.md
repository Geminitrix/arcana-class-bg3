## What each one does that no other does

- **Windborn** (Unbound) — no armour, no weapons; marks every enemy it touches and rushes to the untouched; opens Aeolus' Bag to give the party a wind; its Zephyr ranks up at 4 and 8, the only subclass content on feat levels (WB16).
- **Deceiver** — 1-HP Doubles to spend and Doubt that snowballs into Deluded; Splinters on command and, at Tier III, Doubles that burst when struck; two-step damage (Seed → burst); copies other people's spells; disguises and Friends.
- **Starchild** — the strongest healing; sings one light a day (Stars, Sun, Moon) that shapes every card; answers allies in their darkest hour (Dusk, Nightfall → Dawn); exploration skills by Hymn.
- **Eternal** — heavy armour; spends its own HP as a resource; the only Aspect card that scales with damage taken.
- **Kingmaker** — puts allies and enemies on one turning Wheel; an enemy that falls while an ally reigns suffers a Crossing; names a Court of offices before battle; shines on the other characters' turns.
- **Resonant** — one spell is both buff and debuff; a second emotion poured into a mood reacts by distance on Plutchik's wheel and refunds its slot; feels what it causes (the Overload), then pours it out as Emotional Overload (feelings into damage, Mental Fatigue, a dance).

| | Strongest at | Weakest at |

| Windborn (Unbound) | Mobility, reach to any enemy, party movement and protection (the Open Path, the winds) | Sustain; useless in armour; duels outside a wind |

| Starchild | Support, sustain | Early damage, mobility; needs injured allies to awaken |
| Eternal | Defence, sustain, melee damage | Mobility, support, out-of-combat utility |
| Kingmaker | Party amplification (crits, Advantage, Disadvantage spread over both sides), turning a fight in one turn, a known round | Depends on the party and position; enemies left on the Wheel reign again; allies who reign fall (until Tier III); solo damage low |
| Resonant | Control from level 1, ally buffs with no saves, slot economy through reactions | Needs a turn of setup; Wisdom saves; constructs feel nothing; charm-immune creatures don't dance; *Overloaded* hurts and weakens their mind while it lasts |

## Level rhythm (when each subclass gets cards)

All four get cards at **1, 3, 5, 7, 9, 11** and their Awakening tiers at **3, 7, 12**. From standard v2.1 (ARC-0016, D9) a subclass may use **any** level, odd or even — levels 4 and 8 (feats) stay empty, except the Windborn's Zephyr ranks at 4 and 8 (WB16); each subclass moves to a spread map in its own refining, and its map lives in `21.03/<S>.md`. Levels 13–20
give the base class's capstone passives and, for Deceiver and Eternal, one extra card at 13
(Howl of the Dead). The Windborn's map: `21.03/Unbound.md` (WB43).
