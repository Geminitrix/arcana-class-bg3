# The Windborn (Unbound) — decision log

The display name is **The Windborn** (ARC-0021, Owner, 2026-10-16); ids, folders and this dossier keep the internal
name **Unbound** (ADR-0002). Decisions come from the refining of ARC-0022 (`REFINE_Unbound.md`). Where a later
decision replaces an earlier one, the later one says so; `DESIGN.md` shows only the result.

## Decision log

| # | Decision | Source |
|---|---|---|
| WB0 | Display name **The Windborn** (was The Unbound); internal ids unchanged | Owner, 2026-10-16 (ARC-0021) |
| WB1 | The Windborn is a **monk-like** martial, closest to the **Air Nomads** (*Avatar: The Last Airbender*). Its kit leans on **cards that enable passive states** (and on passives); this is by design, not a gap to fix | Owner, 2026-10-16 |
| WB2 | Axis (`_SUBCLASS_STANDARD.md` §2.3): **Position** — *where is everyone, and where do I go?* | Owner, 2026-10-16 (proposal accepted) |
| WB3 | Core mechanic: **The Open Road** (`REFINE_Unbound.md` §5, direction A) — the **Windswept** mark on each enemy struck; the wind rewards striking an enemy that isn't marked yet; an ultimate that releases every mark at once (working name *Aeolus' Bag*). Rules, reward and names still open | Owner, 2026-10-16 (direction A accepted) |
| WB4 | **Zephyr Strike is the signature button** and ranks up along the **Beaufort scale** bands (Breeze → Gale → Storm → Hurricane); levels and effects of each rank open | Owner, 2026-10-16 (direction B as ranks accepted) |
| WB5 | The wind's reward: **Zephyr Strike against an enemy that isn't Windswept is a Rush** — it carries you to that enemy (range ‹…›) and strikes, with the `Rush_SpringAttack` animation (the one vanilla `Rush_MAG_ZephyrFlash` uses). Against a Windswept enemy it is a plain strike | Owner, 2026-10-16 (option A accepted) |
| WB6 | The **push** (‹…› m) joins Zephyr Strike at the **Gale** rank, not before | Owner, 2026-10-16 |
| WB7 | **Leap of Faith** moves to the `Rush_ForceTunnel` animation (a dash through a line), so the Aspect card and the signature button look different; its VFX are swapped to wind | Owner, 2026-10-16 |
| WB8 | **What marks (the Touch of the Wind):** an enemy becomes **Windswept** when any attack of yours hits it, or when it fails a saving throw against any effect of yours (cards included). Duration, the ally rule and the Awakening alignment still open | Owner, 2026-10-16 (option 1 of 3 accepted) |
| WB9 | **Duration — inhale and exhale:** Windswept lasts **until the end of your next turn**. One turn builds marks, the next releases them (the ultimate) or moves on to enemies not yet marked; you can't Rush the same enemy two turns in a row. Marks from reactions count. Rule for small fights (every standing enemy marked) still open | Owner, 2026-10-16 (option 2 of 3; "the full circle" and its variants 3A–3C weighed and set aside) |
| WB10 | **The Open Path:** a Windswept enemy **can't make opportunity attacks** — the wind opens the road for the whole party (answers DM-M5, DM-P3). The current Tier I rule "moving doesn't provoke opportunity attacks" becomes redundant and is replaced when the tiers are designed | Owner, 2026-10-16 (option 1 of 3; "allies strike with the wind" and "pure mark, allies at Tier II" set aside) |
| WB11 | **The ultimate is Aeolus' Bag of the four winds** (working name): a **bonus action**, support and lore, meant to feel **epic**. You open the bag and **choose one wind** (the Anemoi — North, South, East, West), each with a boon for allies and a bane for enemies (table in `REFINE_Unbound.md`, values ‹…›). It blows for **X turns, X = enemies Windswept when you open it**, and the bag can't be opened again until it stops. Arrives around **level 5** (level open). Replaces WB3's "a gust on every marked enemy". Open: whether opening spends the marks, the case X = 0, a cap on X, the wind list and effects | Owner, 2026-10-16 (option B of 3; "prevailing wind at combat start" and "winds wake with the Awakening tiers" set aside) |
| WB12 | **Exhale:** opening the bag **clears every Windswept mark** (the road reopens for the Rush; the Open Path pauses until you mark again). While the wind blows, its **bane falls on Windswept enemies**; its boon is for allies | Owner, 2026-10-16 (option 1 of 3; "the wind only reads" and "caught in the wind" set aside) |
| WB13 | **Limits of X:** the bag needs **at least one Windswept enemy** to open. X is capped by the **Beaufort rank of Zephyr Strike** — Breeze ‹…›, Gale ‹…›, Storm ‹…›, Hurricane ‹…› — so each rank announces a longer wind, and the epic peak is the Hurricane at level 12 | Owner, 2026-10-16 (option 2 of 3; "always opens, fixed cap" and "no cap, rest of X turns" set aside) |
| WB14 | Aeolus' Bag arrives at **level 2** (fills an empty level; level 1 teaches the mark and the Rush). Replaces "around level 5" of WB11 | Owner, 2026-10-16 (recommendation accepted) |
| WB15 | **The winds join the bag with the ranks.** **Zephyrus is yours** — the free wind of the myth, Zephyr Strike — and stays out of the bag. **Breeze** (the bag's arrival): **Boreas** (movement) and **Notus** (protection). **Gale:** **Eurus** joins as the **attack** wind (melee and ranged). **Hurricane:** the bag releases **two winds at once**. Values ‹…›; Eurus weighed against the others so it isn't always the right choice (DM-B5) | Owner, 2026-10-16 (option 3 of 3; "four winds from the start" and "three winds from the start" set aside) |
| WB16 | **Zephyr's ranks by level:** Breeze 1 · **Gale 4** · **Storm 8** · Hurricane 12. **Exception** to `_SUBCLASS_STANDARD.md` §6.3 / DM-J8 ("levels 4 and 8 carry no subclass content", ARC-0016 D9) for the Windborn only. Reason: a rank upgrades a button the player already uses (a predictable concept, §6.5), so it sits beside the feat choice without overload, and levels 6 and 10 stay free for cards. Vanilla precedent: `Progressions.lsx` gives subclass content at 4 and 8 (Way of the Four Elements picks a new discipline at 4 and 8; Cleric domains' Divine Strike / Potent Spellcasting at 8; Totem Warrior's Land's Stride at 8) | Owner, 2026-10-16 (Owner's proposal; Gale 6 / Storm 10 set aside) |
| WB17 | **Storm rank — the wind doesn't stop:** after Zephyr Strike hits an enemy that wasn't Windswept, you may make **one more Rush** to another unmarked enemy within ‹…› m, free, the same turn (once per Zephyr). Pattern: a one-turn created card granted by a status, like today's `ZEPHYR_STRIKE` | Owner, 2026-10-16 (option 1 of 3; "the storm's thunder" and "strike and return" set aside) |
| WB18 | **No exception for small fights:** when every standing enemy is Windswept, Zephyr Strike is a plain strike (no Rush); **opening the bag is what reopens the road**. In a duel the bag becomes the heart of the fight (short, frequent winds) | Owner, 2026-10-16 (option 3 of 3; "Rush any when all are marked" and "the road renews itself at turn start" set aside) |
| WB19 | **Role — balanced hybrid, like the air monk:** a **mobile striker and a party supporter in equal measure** (versatile, not a specialist). Damage comes from the hands and the Rush; support from the Open Path, Aeolus' Bag and cards that carry allies with the wind. The kit isn't tilted to either side; the balance pass sets the budget split ‹…› and the benchmark (Monk for the striker share, a support class for the rest) | Owner, 2026-10-16 (a mix of options 1 and 3; "field-shaping skirmisher" set aside) |
| WB20 | **Chassis — the monk's two abilities:** the Windborn's cards use a **Dexterity save DC** — vanilla `ManeuverSaveDC()` = 8 + proficiency + max(STR, DEX) (`Shared/Scripts/thoth/helpers/CommonConditions.khn`). Be the Leaf stays **AC = 10 + DEX + WIS**. Intelligence stays the class's casting ability for the base-class utilities (Weaver Hand). Preset to favour **DEX and WIS** (closes `10.06` #12 for the Windborn). Saves DEX, WIS; no armour, no shield, unarmed; Monk sound set; yellow — unchanged. **Exception** to the class line "every subclass casts with Intelligence" (`05.01` §2): the class text and the base-class impact are noted for propagation | Owner, 2026-10-16 (option 3 of 3; "INT in the AC" and "keep DEX/WIS/INT" set aside) |
| WB21 | **Replaces WB20. Chassis — the seer reads the blow:** Be the Leaf becomes **AC = 10 + DEX + INT**. The cards keep the class's **Intelligence** casting (no exception to `05.01` §2). The Windborn's two main abilities are **DEX and INT**; preset to favour them (closes `10.06` #12 for the Windborn). Saves DEX, WIS; no armour, no shield, unarmed; Monk sound set; yellow — unchanged | Owner, 2026-10-16 (option 1 of 3, after first choosing option 3 as WB20) |
| WB22 | **Awakening goal — marks released:** the Aspect awakens by counting **Windswept marks the wind releases**, cumulative within the fight (each mark cleared by opening the bag counts 1; goals per tier ‹…›). Progress shown as a counter (ARC-0007 pattern). Whether the wind also sweeps marks each turn while it blows: under discussion | Owner, 2026-10-16 (option 3 of 3; "marks in one round" and "Rushes in the fight" set aside) |
| WB23 | **The wind sweeps:** while a wind blows, **at the start of your turn it sweeps every Windswept mark away**, and each swept mark counts for the Awakening (WB22). Opening the bag is the first sweep (WB12). Marks therefore work through the whole round (Open Path, the bane) and the road reopens each turn of wind. The sweep doesn't extend the wind (X is fixed when the bag opens). Outside a wind, WB9 holds | Owner, 2026-10-16 (Owner's idea; start of turn instead of end of turn, and only while a wind blows, accepted) |
