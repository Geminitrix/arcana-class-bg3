# The Starchild — decision log

Decisions come from the refining of ARC-0023 (`REFINE_Starchild.md`). Where a later decision replaces an earlier one,
the later one says so; `DESIGN.md` shows only the result.

## Decision log

| # | Decision | Source |
|---|---|---|
| SC0 | The Starchild is a **healer first** — high, focused healing (Soraka), with support second. Every light keeps healing as the verb | Owner, 2026-10-16 (brief) |
| SC1 | **One light changes every heal:** the Starchild takes one of three lights — **Star** (Radiant, focused healing), **Sun** (Fire), **Moon** (Cold) — and that light changes what **every** heal of hers does; it ranks up with the Awakening tiers. Cards stay shared. Moonlit / Sunlit leave the Awakening and become the Moon and Sun lights (fixes DM-A2). Exclusive cards per light may come later. Working term "path" is retired (name open) | Owner, 2026-10-16 (option C of 3; "fork Sun or Moon on a Star trunk" and "three lights card by card" set aside) |
| SC2 | **Chosen once per long rest:** after a long rest she has no light; she chooses one, gains a visible status naming it, and keeps it until the next long rest. Free to change day by day | Owner, 2026-10-16 (Owner proposal, endorsed) |
| SC3 | **Names — the Hymns:** the choice is a **Hymn**; the action is **Sing a Hymn**; the three lights are **Hymn of the Stars**, **Hymn of the Sun**, **Hymn of the Moon**. Grounding to verify for S6/S7: the Orphic Hymns to the Stars, to Helios and to Selene. Set aside: Prayer (vanilla *Prayer of Healing*, cleric flavour), Invoke (deck keyword), Oath (Paladin), Vigil, Vow | Owner, 2026-10-16 |
| SC4 | **The three Hymns must be equally viable and interesting** — each wins some days and some fights (DM-B5); balanced together in one pass | Owner, 2026-10-16 |
| SC5 | **No Hymn = Unsung:** after a long rest, until she sings, she has the visible status **Unsung** — *"Your light flickers, dim and waiting. Sing a Hymn to choose your light."* A reminder only, no penalty (DM-P11): her heals are simply plain. Set aside: Dim Light (reads as BG3's light/obscurity system), Silent Sky | Owner, 2026-10-16 (name of option 2, text of option 1) |
| SC6 | **The Hymns change the shape of the heal** (all three heal strongly): **Stars — focused** (all on one ally, the largest single heal); **Sun — radiant** (the target is healed, a share ‹…› spills to nearby allies, the light burns enemies next to the target); **Moon — tidal** (part now, the rest at the start of the next ‹2› turns; whoever attacks the ally is chilled and slowed). Each wins a recognisable fight: Stars a boss or focused damage, Sun spread damage and grouped enemies, Moon long fights and steady pressure on the tank. Fire and Cold are seasoning, not the focus. Brake: the Sun total over several allies must not beat the Stars on one ally | Owner, 2026-10-16 (option C of 3; "same heal, different rider" and "heal traded for rider" set aside) |
| SC7 | **Axis: Timing** — *when do I heal, and whom?* Hope shines brightest in the darkest hour (the Star after the Tower): a heal on an ally in danger (below ‹half› HP) is stronger. The Hymns answer the question differently — the Moon heals before the danger, the Stars in it, the Sun reaches several near it. Astral Infusion keeps its HP cost as a cost, not as the axis. Feasibility: vanilla `HasHPPercentageLessThan(n)` (Gustav `Status_BOOST.txt:1903`, Shared `Spell_Shout.txt:2935`) | Owner, 2026-10-16 (option A of 3; "Resource conversion — pay with your own HP" and "Accumulation — seven stars and the great star" set aside) |
| SC8 | **The darkest hour is an exclusive mark** (working name): when an ally drops below ‹half› HP, they gain a visible status; the Starchild's next heal on that ally **consumes it** — the heal is ‹stronger› and the current Hymn's effect is ‹reinforced›. Other cards may read the mark (Starbreath, Wish, the ultimate). The Starchild's own state (fixes DM-M1, M3, K1). Applied by an Osiris rule on crossing the threshold (pattern: the Awakening's HitpointsChanged). Steps of option B may be added later if the tension is weak in test. Open: name, duration, what removes it, whether she can mark herself | Owner, 2026-10-16 (option C of 3; "simple threshold" and "two steps" set aside) |
| SC9 | **The mark is named Dusk** — the hour the light fades and the stars come out, where the three lights meet. Draft text: *"Dusk falls on this ally. The Starchild's next heal answers it."* Set aside: Darkest Hour, Falling Star | Owner, 2026-10-16 (option 3 of 3, "more poetic") |
| SC10 | **Dusk and Nightfall, by depth — never both:** below ‹half› HP an ally is in **Dusk**; below ‹a quarter›, in **Nightfall** (it replaces Dusk; one StackId). Her heal consumes either; a heal on Nightfall is stronger than on Dusk and brings the Hymn's effect to its peak (the answer = *Dawn*, working name). Lifecycle (option A of SC9's follow-up, endorsed): a mark appears on each **downward crossing** of a threshold; rising above ‹a quarter› turns Nightfall into Dusk, rising above ‹half› removes the mark (any source of healing); after her heal consumes it, no new mark until the next downward crossing. Open: Downed allies, the Starchild herself, the level Nightfall arrives (‹5–7› suggested) | Owner, 2026-10-16 (Nightfall by depth chosen over "after 2 turns unanswered"; Owner set mutual exclusion) |
| SC11 | **Downed clears the mark.** Dusk and Nightfall are for allies still standing; raising a Downed ally is a plain heal (reviving the dead stays with Starbreath). The reward is catching the ally between ‹0› and ‹half› HP before they fall — the Timing axis; it also keeps BG3's "yo-yo" revive from becoming the dominant play (DM-B5, I7). No exception to SC10's table: Downed is not "below a quarter" for the mark | Owner, 2026-10-16 (option B of 3; "Downed keeps Nightfall" and "Downed becomes Dusk" set aside) |
| SC12 | **The Starchild herself, in two steps.** Early levels: only allies can be marked (Dusk is her gaze outward, like Soraka). At a later level ‹…› (a new rule, a felt level-up — DM-J1): she can fall into Dusk / Nightfall too; her own mark is **not** consumed by heals and, while she carries it, **the heals she gives are ‹stronger›** — the light shines brightest when she is in the dark. This ties Astral Infusion's HP cost to the mechanic (paying HP can push her into Dusk; DM-M3) and borrows the Risk axis for one decision. Brake: her bonus plus the target's mark never exceed the Dawn heal | Owner, 2026-10-16 (option C of 3, arriving later, with option B early; "marked like any ally" set aside) |
| SC13 | **The ultimate is the answered wish** (working name *Wish*; the current Wish card becomes it): heals **every ally** in a large area and **answers every mark at once** — Nightfall → Dawn, Dusk → the strong heal, unmarked → a plain heal. It reads the state (DM-K7): the darker the field, the bigger the answer. No new button (DM-X1). Level, area and values open | Owner, 2026-10-16 (option A of 3) |
| SC14 | **The Great Star** (an eight-pointed star over the field that answers marks for ‹N› turns) and **Eclipse** (the three Hymns at once for ‹1–2› turns) are kept as candidates for other pieces of the progression (cards, Aspect card, Awakening tiers) | Owner, 2026-10-16 |
| SC15 | **Rename every borrowed name.** Names taken from Soraka and Lux (*Starcall, Astral Infusion, Rejuvenation, Equinox, Wish, Lucent Singularity, Final Spark*) are replaced by names of the subclass's own: The Star (XVII), the Aspect of Hope, the Sun and the Moon. Done in the names pass, after the kit is settled. Same path as the Deceiver (DV29) | Owner, 2026-10-16 |
| SC16 | **Star alone, Sun and Moon together.** The **Aspect card is the Great Star** (from SC14), and it belongs to the **Stars only**: it answers marks with the Stars' focused heal, whatever Hymn was sung. **Tier III is Eclipse**: the **Sun and the Moon** meet — until combat ends her heals both radiate (Sun) and come as a tide (Moon); the Stars are not part of it. The three are never mixed in one piece. Balance note: the Great Star adds most for a Sun or Moon singer, Eclipse adds most for a Stars singer — check in the balance pass that this evens out (DM-B5; Tier III comes later and less often than Tier I). Replaces *Wish Upon a Star* and the Tier III ability bonus | Owner, 2026-10-16 (option 1 of 3, with the Star / Sun-and-Moon split set by the Owner) |
| SC17 | **Awakening goal: answered marks.** Answering a Dusk counts 1, a Nightfall counts 2; tier goals ‹3 / 5 / 8› are placeholders for the balance pass. Hope wakes only when the party is in danger — easy fights don't need it; the player is tempted to let allies sink to raise the tension, braked by SC11 (a Downed ally loses the mark). Replaces "heal 5× / 8× / 12× level" (2026-10-02); the countdown status (ARC-0007) switches from HEAL to COUNT | Owner, 2026-10-16 (option B of 3; "effective healing" and "healing on a mark counts double" set aside) |
| SC18 | **Tier I and Tier II.** **Tier I:** the Great Star card (SC16) and **Undying Hope** — once per fight, the first ally who would drop to 0 HP stays at 1 HP **in Nightfall** (vanilla Death Ward pattern), so she can answer it with Dawn. **Tier II:** an ally she heals applies a **condition, no damage**, to the next enemy they hit, by the Hymn sung: **Stars — Dazzled** (−1d4 to attack rolls only; vanilla Bane is `RollBonus(Attack,-1d4)` plus saves), **Moon — a strong slow** (half movement; vanilla Ray of Frost is only `ActionResource(Movement,-3,0)`), **Sun — Burning** (vanilla, 1d4 Fire per turn). Fire resistance is the Sun's weakness and a reason for the daily choice (SC2). Duration ‹1–2› turns and growth to level 12 in the balance pass (DM-B6). Replaces Moonlit / Sunlit (same OnAttack pattern) | Owner, 2026-10-16 (Owner's idea, refined: conditions instead of 1d4 damage; Moon slow stronger than Ray of Frost) |
| SC19 | **The first heal of the turn.** Tier II's condition and Tier III's Eclipse apply only to the **first heal she casts on a single ally** each turn. Area heals (the ultimate, the Sun's spill) and the Moon's tide pulses never count. **Tier III — Eclipse, amended:** that first heal joins **all three** shapes (focused, radiant, tidal) and passes **all three** conditions (Dazzled, strong slow, Burning) — in an eclipse the stars come out by day. **Amends SC16:** Eclipse is no longer Sun and Moon only; the Great Star stays the Stars' alone. **Once-per-turn gate (Owner):** the empowered heal applies a hidden status to the Starchild lasting 1 turn, and the effect checks she doesn't have it. Build note: give it `TickType StartTurn` so it lasts until her next turn begins (a default end-of-turn tick with duration 1 would expire the same turn — the Wind Whispers lesson, 2026-09-30); vanilla `OncePerTurn` resets on every combatant's turn, so the status is the right gate. The rule is in effect once per round. Price the best case (first heal on a Nightfall ally under Eclipse = Dawn + three shapes + three conditions) in the balance pass (DM-B2) | Owner, 2026-10-16 (Owner's proposal, with the hidden-status gate set by the Owner) |
| SC20 | **Six vanilla twins replaced** (`REFINE_Starchild.md` §5, SG1–SG6; working names): Solar Purge → **Sunwash** (cleanse; on a marked ally consumes the mark, removes every condition, small heal) · Lunar Aegis → **Moon Halo** (bonus action, ‹3› turns, no Concentration, ‹+AC› only while in Dusk / Nightfall) · Moon Gravity → **Moonpull** (pull an ally ‹X› m, no opportunity attacks; on a marked ally ends Stun / Restrained / Paralysed) · Starbreath → **Rekindle** (revive in Nightfall, long-rest cooldown) · Astral Fortitude → **Long Twilight** (allies within ‹6› m: Dusk begins at ‹⅔›) · Star Radiance → **Guiding Star** (marked enemy; allies in Dusk / Nightfall who hit it regain ‹X› HP) | Owner, 2026-10-16 (all six approved) |
| SC21 | **The Hymn on every card: the Stars sharpen, the Sun spreads, the Moon lingers** — Stars more on the one target, Sun more targets / area / fire, Moon longer / over time / slow-cold; the per-card variants of `REFINE_Starchild.md` §5.1 approved. Set aside: a bespoke effect per card and Hymn | Owner, 2026-10-16 |
| SC22 | **Build: one container per card.** Each card is a spell container with **three children** — a Stars spell, a Sun spell, a Moon spell — **locked by the Hymn sung**; same icon, a different name per light, a tooltip for that light only. Proven in the mod: the Hemoplague container (cost on the children, CardDB maps child → parent) and `gen_upcast_variants.pl`'s container handling (`ContainerSpells` / `SpellContainerID`). Probe (T2): a child greyed out by a `HasStatus('<HYMN>', context.Source)` requirement. Replaces the "IF branches / UnlockSpellVariant" options of §5.1 | Owner, 2026-10-16 |
| SC23 | **Unsung, with containers:** while Unsung every card's three children are locked; **Sing a Hymn can be used in combat as a bonus action** while she is Unsung (still once per long rest). Forgetting the morning ritual costs the bonus action of her first turn, never the turn. **Amends SC5** ("no penalty" → "costs one bonus action"). Set aside: a fourth, lightless child; an automatic default Hymn | Owner, 2026-10-16 (option A of 3) |
| SC24 | **The heal cards use the same container** (a child per Hymn, SC22), so the whole kit reads one way | Claude's proposal, 2026-10-16 — the Owner did not object; confirm at the design gate |
| SC25 | **Card review part 2 approved** (`REFINE_Starchild.md` §6, SG7–SG11): **Starcall** kept as strike and fallback (Rejuvenation ‹+1› turn next to a marked ally) · **Astral Infusion** is the signature button in three ranks — I heals and consumes Dusk (1), II answers Nightfall with Dawn (‹5–7›), III her own Dusk empowers it (‹9–11›) · Lunar Wisp → **Hesperus** (card-passive, Unique: a reaction that answers a new mark at once with a ‹small› heal) · Stellar Grace → **Dawnbringer** (‹N› turns: a heal on Dusk answers as Nightfall; long-rest cooldown, Unique) · the ultimate's Hymn variants. Each with the Hymn variants of the table | Owner, 2026-10-16 (all approved) |
