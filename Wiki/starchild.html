The Starchild

We keep a light burning after every fall, and the Weave lets us pour it into the wounded.

A Starchild

Mystra wove the Starchild from the light that remains after disaster, a thread of hope that never stops shining. They mend wounds that should have been fatal, shelter their allies beneath solar and lunar wards, and call the fallen back from the edge of death. The Starchild is the Arcana's healer. You strike with starlight to fuel your own renewal, pour that light into wounded allies, and raise those who fall. Late in your journey the same light burns, blinds and silences whole groups of enemies.

The Card

The seventeenth card of the Major Arcana shows a figure kneeling at the edge of a pool beneath a night sky. They pour water from two jugs: one into the pool, one onto the dry earth beside it. Above them a great star burns, ringed by seven smaller ones.

The Star follows The Tower, the card of sudden ruin. That is why it reads as hope: the storm has passed, the walls have fallen, and something is still shining. It is the card of healing after the worst has happened, of renewal that asks for nothing back, and of a light that shows the way home.

This is the Starchild's destiny. One jug pours into the wounded; the other waters the ground the party stands on, as wards of sun and moon. The great star is the light itself, gentle on allies and merciless on anyone who tries to put it out.

Upright

  • Hope
  • Healing
  • Renewal
  • Guidance

You keep the party standing: heals, wards and revivals that arrive when the fight has turned against you.

Reversed

  • Despair
  • Lost faith
  • Disconnection

Enemies meet the other face of the light: Blinded, Silenced, Stunned and set Burning, cut off from the fight.

Playing a Starchild

You are the party's healer and support. A typical turn starts with Starcall on an enemy: it deals Radiant damage, slows the target and grants you Rejuvenation. Your bonus action then goes to Astral Infusion, which heals an ally without costing you any hit points while Rejuvenation lasts. Around that rhythm you place wards (Lunar Aegis, Moon Gravity, Astral Fortitude), cleanse conditions with Solar Purge, and bring the fallen back with Starbreath. From level 5 you also shape the battlefield with light, and at level 11 you gain two of the hardest-hitting area spells in the class.

At a Glance

  • Role: healer and support, with area control late on.
  • Strengths: single-target and group healing, revival, a cleanse for most conditions, Armour Class and hit point wards, medium armour and shields.
  • Weaknesses: modest damage until level 11; five key spells need Concentration; Astral Infusion costs your own hit points without Rejuvenation; the Aspect of Hope only grows when allies get hurt.
  • Tip: cast Lunar Wisp before a fight. It needs no action and stays until combat ends, so every heal in that fight also grants temporary hit points.

The Healing Loop

Your two level 1 spells are built to be cast together. Starcall takes your action: a ranged spell attack that deals Radiant damage, reduces the target's movement speed for 1 turn and gives you Rejuvenation for 2 turns. Rejuvenation increases your movement speed by 30% and restores hit points equal to your Wisdom modifier at the end of each turn.

Astral Infusion takes your bonus action and heals an ally within 9m. Normally it also costs you 10% of your maximum hit points, and you can't cast it at all while you are at 11% of your hit points or lower. While you have Rejuvenation, that cost disappears: the heal costs only the Woven Spell Slot.

Every heal you give an ally feeds two other things. With Lunar Wisp active, the healed ally also gains temporary hit points equal to your Arcana level plus twice your Wisdom modifier. From level 3, the hit points you restore to allies in combat count towards the Aspect of Hope.

One Turn, in Order

  • Action: Starcall an enemy. On a hit you gain Rejuvenation.
  • Bonus action: Astral Infusion on the ally who needs it most, with no hit point cost.
  • End of turn: Rejuvenation restores your Wisdom modifier in hit points.

Starcall grants Rejuvenation only when it hits. If it misses, Infusion still works, but it takes its 10% from you.

Sun, Moon and Stars

The Starchild's light takes three forms. Solar light purges and burns: it clears conditions from allies and sets enemies alight. Lunar light shelters: it hardens Armour Class, wraps healing in temporary hit points and keeps allies moving. Starlight heals, revives and reveals. The Aspect of Hope follows the same pattern, marking allies as Moonlit, then Sunlit, then shielding them with an Eclipse.

Concentration

Five of your spells need Concentration: Lunar Aegis, Lucent Singularity, Star Radiance, Final Spark and Stellar Grace. You can hold only one at a time, so choose the one that matters most in this fight. Stellar Anchor gives you Advantage on Saving Throws to keep it when you take damage.

Aspect of Hope

From level 3, every fight can awaken the Star within you. What counts is the healing you give: each hit point you restore to an ally during combat adds to a running total for that fight. Healing yourself doesn't count, and neither does healing past an ally's maximum hit points. Your progress towards the next tier appears as the Awakening Progress status, shown in percent.

Each goal is a multiple of your level. When your total reaches a goal, that tier awakens and stays until combat ends. Tiers awaken in order and stack, and you can only reach the tiers your level has unlocked: Tier I at level 3, Tier II at level 7, Tier III at level 12. When combat ends, the total and every tier reset.

Once awakened, the Aspect turns your support into damage. Every ally you heal, and every ally you target with an Arcane spell, receives the marks of your current tier for 2 turns. Each mark empowers that ally's next attack that hits, then fades.

Tier I · Beacon

  • Allies you heal or target become Moonlit: their next attack that hits deals an additional 1d4 Cold and reduces the target's movement speed by 3m for 1 turn.
  • You gain the spell Wish Upon a Star.

  • Allies also become Sunlit: their next attack that hits deals an additional 1d4 Fire and sets the target Burning for 2 turns.
