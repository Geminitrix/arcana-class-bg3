The Unbound

We leap before we look, and the Weave catches us wherever we land.

An Unbound

Mystra wove the Unbound from the first step of every journey, a thread that answers only to curiosity and the open road. They wear no armour and strike with open hands and moving air, slipping past every blow until the ground itself stops holding them down. The Unbound is the Arcana's martial destiny: a fast, unarmoured striker who fights with fists and wind and turns every spell into one more blow. Pick it if you want to cross the battlefield in a single turn, reach every enemy before they act, and stay alive by never being where the hit lands.

The Card

The Fool is card 0 of the Major Arcana. Every other Arcanum has a number that places it somewhere along the road; the Fool's zero places it before the road begins, which is why it can stand at the start of any journey. The card shows a young traveller at the edge of a cliff, a small bundle slung over one shoulder and a white flower in hand, a little dog at their heels. They are looking up at the sky, not down at the drop.

Read in a spread, the Fool means beginnings, freedom and trust: the step taken before you know where it leads, by someone who carries nothing that could hold them back. The traveller is not ignorant of the cliff. They simply believe the world will catch them.

That is the Unbound's destiny. Mystra wove them from the first step of every journey, so they travel light in the most literal sense: no armour, no weapon, nothing but open hands and the wind. In combat their curiosity pulls them towards every enemy on the field, and the Aspect of Curiosity awakens when they have touched as many of them as they can in a single round.

Playing an Unbound

You are a skirmisher who fights in melee without armour or weapons. A typical turn: cross the field with a movement speed no one else matches, hit with an unarmed attack (two from level 5), then spend your bonus action on a Zephyr Strike, the extra blow that follows every unarmed attack and every Arcane spell you cast. Your spells mostly strengthen you rather than the party: more bonus actions, more damage on your fists, flight, a reaction against arrows. You survive by not being hit. Dexterity and Wisdom both raise your Armour Class, enemies attack you at Disadvantage, and from level 12 every hit that lands deals only half damage.

At a Glance

  • Role: unarmoured unarmed striker and skirmisher
  • Strengths: the best mobility in the class, many attacks per turn, layered evasion, strong exploration (flight, long jumps, no falling damage)
  • Weaknesses: any armour or shield switches your features off; no healing beyond temporary hit points; little area damage or control
  • Tip: raise Dexterity first, then Wisdom. Be the Leaf adds both to your Armour Class, and Dexterity drives every unarmed attack and Storm Fists.

Open Hands

You fight with your fists, and three passives you gain at level 1 make them as good as any weapon. Flowing Strikes: Agility lets your unarmed attacks use Dexterity instead of Strength when your Dexterity is higher. Flowing Strikes: Precision sets the damage die of your unarmed attacks, which grows with your character level. From level 7, Magic Strikes makes them magical.

Zephyr Strike

The engine of the subclass is Flowing Strikes: Zephyr. Whenever you make an unarmed attack, cast an Arcane spell or cast True Strike, you can make a Zephyr Strike as a bonus action for the rest of that turn: one more unarmed attack against an enemy within reach. Every Unbound spell ends with the same line, "Afterwards, you can make a Zephyr Strike as a bonus action." A Zephyr Strike is itself an unarmed attack, so as long as you have bonus actions left, the next one is ready.

That makes bonus actions your real currency. You have one each turn, and these features add more:

  • Flow State (level 3): one additional bonus action each turn while it lasts, two when cast with a level 5 or 6 slot.
  • Restless Wind (Aspect Tier II, level 7): once per turn, a Zephyr Strike doesn't cost a bonus action.

Several Unbound spells (Wind Whispers, Storm Fists, Evasive Flow, Thunderclap) are cast as a bonus action themselves. Casting one still opens a Zephyr Strike, but you need a second bonus action to take it. From level 5, Flowing Assault gives your attack action a second, free attack.

A Level 5 Turn

With Flow State active from your previous turn: take the Attack action and hit with your fist, then make the free attack from Flowing Assault. Spend your bonus action on a Zephyr Strike, and the extra bonus action from Flow State on a second one. That is four unarmed attacks, each dealing 1d6 + your Dexterity modifier, and four chances to strike four different enemies for the Aspect of Curiosity.

Moving Air

The Unbound carries nothing that could hold them down. Every Unbound passive, spell and effect works only while you wear no armour and carry no shield. Clothing such as the Unbound's Robe is fine. Equipping armour or a shield switches your passives off and ends your active Unbound effects at once.

Armour Class

Be the Leaf sets your Armour Class to 10 + your Dexterity modifier + your Wisdom modifier. It doesn't stack with Mage Armour. Two passives add +2 more while their spell is active: Eye of the Storm (Untethered) and Momentum (Thunderclap).

Speed and the fall

Twinkle Toes raises your movement speed at almost every odd level, all the way to level 20. Feather Fall, from level 5, cuts your Falling damage and multiplies your Jump distance until you are Immune to falling at level 12.

Aspect of Curiosity

At level 3 you gain 0 - The Fool: Awakening. In combat, curiosity pulls you towards every enemy on the field: strike different enemies with attacks within a single round, and the Aspect of Curiosity awakens in you until combat ends.

  • What counts: any attack that hits an enemy. Unarmed attacks, Zephyr Strikes, Wind Riposte, a missile hurled back with Gale Deflection and attack spells all count.
  • The round runs from the start of your turn to the start of your next one, so hits you land with reactions during enemy turns count too.
  • Few enemies left: if fewer enemies stand when your turn begins than a goal asks for, every hit counts towards that goal, even on the same enemy. A duel can still awaken you.
  • Awakening Progress, a status on your portrait, shows the hits that count towards your next tier this round. It resets when your turn begins.
  • Each goal is checked on its own, so a single busy round can awaken several tiers at once. Every tier you reach stays until combat ends, and each one adds to the last.

Conditions & Terms

Arcane spell
The Arcana's combat spells cast with Woven Spell Slots: every Unbound spell on this page and most Weaver Hand spells. Utility spells such as Arcane Recovery don't count. Each one you cast opens a Zephyr Strike.
No armour
Every Unbound feature works only while you wear no armour and carry no shield. Equipping either switches your passives off and ends your active Unbound effects.
Zephyr Strike
An unarmed attack made as a bonus action. Available for the rest of the turn after an unarmed attack, an Arcane spell or True Strike.
Wind Riposte
While Wind Whispers is active: when an enemy within 2m misses you with a melee attack, you use your reaction to strike back with an unarmed attack.
Gale Deflection: Redirect
After Gale Deflection reduces a missile's damage to 0, bend the air around the projectile and hurl it back at your attacker as an unarmed attack.
Opening
From Evasive Flow: Advantage on your next unarmed Attack Roll.
Prone
Knocked to the ground. The creature has Disadvantage on Strength and Dexterity Saving Throws, attacks against it from within 3m have Advantage, and it must spend half its movement speed to stand up.
Off Balance
Attack Rolls against the creature have Advantage, and it has Disadvantage on Strength and Dexterity Checks. Lasts 1 turn; removed when it takes damage or is Helped.
Reverberation
-1 to Strength, Dexterity and Constitution Saving Throws per remaining turn. At 5 or more turns, the creature takes 1d4 Thunder damage and possibly falls Prone, then the condition ends. Creatures Immune to Thunder damage can't receive it.
Flight
From Untethered: you can Fly to a target position. Ends when combat ends, or when combat begins if you cast it beforehand.
Awakening Progress
Shows the hits that count towards your next Aspect tier this round. Resets when your turn begins.
0 - The Fool: Awakened I / II / III
The status you carry while the Aspect of Curiosity is awakened. It lists the bonuses of every tier you have reached and lasts until combat ends.
Fool's Luck
Aspect Tier III: once per round, the first attack against you misses.
