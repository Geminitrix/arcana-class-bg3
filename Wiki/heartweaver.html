The Heartweaver

We feel what you feel before you do, and the Weave lets us turn it into something else.

A Heartweaver

Mystra wove the Heartweaver from the feelings that pass between every living thing, a thread that tempers one emotion with another. They surge joy, trust or fear into a creature's Weave, then braid what lingers into stronger feelings that lift allies and break enemies from within. The Heartweaver deals little damage of their own. They win by deciding what everyone on the field feels, and in what order: a surge now, a mood that lingers, and a second surge that turns the two into something stronger.

The Card

Temperance is the fourteenth card of the Major Arcana. It shows a winged figure with one foot in a stream and one on dry land, pouring water from one cup into another. The stream runs at an angle no water should follow, and not a drop is spilled. Nothing is added and nothing is lost: the two cups simply trade what they hold until they balance.

The card stands for moderation, patience and the art of blending. It is the alchemist's card, the slow work of mixing two things until they become a third. It follows Death in the Major Arcana, and its lesson is what comes after an ending: mixing again, gently, until the measure is right.

That is the Heartweaver's destiny. They do not create feelings out of nothing. They pour one emotion into a creature that already holds another, and the two become something new. Joy poured into trust becomes Love. Fear poured into surprise becomes Awe. Pour a feeling into its opposite and the cup cracks.

Upright

  • Balance
  • Moderation
  • Patience
  • Blending
  • Healing

Your allies feel the right thing at the right moment. Trust steadies them, joy sharpens them, and the feelings you braid from both carry them through the fight.

Reversed

  • Excess
  • Imbalance
  • Clashing extremes

Your enemies feel too much, all at once. Their emotions overflow into Terror and Grief, and opposites forced together snap into Catharsis.

Playing a Heartweaver

The Heartweaver is a controller and a support caster. Each Weave Surge fills a creature with one emotion for a single turn. When that emotion fades it leaves a milder mood behind, and the next Surge you cast on that creature reacts with the mood instead of landing on its own. Your turns alternate between laying moods and spending them: freeze an enemy with Fear now, then turn its lingering apprehension into Awe or Despair. The same rules work on your allies, so a single Surge can shut an enemy down on one side of the field and lift a friend on the other.

At a Glance

  • Role: controller and support. Low damage, with spikes of Catharsis.
  • Strengths: strong control from level 1 spells, buffs and debuffs from the same spell, more targets with every slot level, and allies never need to roll against you.
  • Weaknesses: needs a turn of setup, enemies resist with Wisdom Saving Throws, and creatures with Intelligence 4 or lower feel nothing at all.
  • Tip: Surge an ally with Trust before the fight turns ugly. Next turn it is Accepting, and a Weave Surge: Joy braids it into Love.

The Wheel of Emotions

Heartweavers teach that every feeling a mind can hold is spun from eight threads: Joy, Trust, Fear, Surprise, Sadness, Disgust, Anger and Anticipation. Laid out in a circle, each thread sits beside its closest kin and directly across from its opposite. Joy faces Sadness, Trust faces Disgust, Fear faces Anger, and Surprise faces Anticipation.

Every thread can be pulled gently or hard. Pulled gently, it becomes a mood, a soft echo that lingers. Pulled hard, it becomes overwhelming. Two threads that touch braid into a feeling, and the farther apart they sit, the stranger that feeling is.

Surge, mood, reaction

Everything the Heartweaver does follows the same three steps.

  1. Surge. A Weave Surge fills a creature with one emotion for 1 turn, such as Emotion: Fear. This is the peak, and it is the strongest effect a single Surge has.
  2. Mood. When the emotion fades on its own, it leaves the milder echo of itself behind for 5 turns: Fear leaves the creature Apprehensive. If the emotion is removed early, it leaves no mood.
  3. Reaction. Cast another Weave Surge on a creature that carries a mood and the two threads react instead of surging. The mood is spent, and what you get depends on how far apart the two emotions sit on the wheel.

A creature feels one emotion at a time and carries one mood at a time; a new one replaces the old. Feelings are different: each one is its own thread, so a creature can carry several different feelings at once.

Every Weave Surge

  • Is a level 1 Enchantment spell cast as an action, with a range of 18m.
  • Touches one creature, plus one more for every slot level above 1: 3 creatures with a level 3 slot, 6 with a level 6 slot.
  • Can target any creature that carries a mood, friend or foe. A creature without a mood can only receive Joy, Trust and Anticipation if it is your ally (or you), and only Fear, Surprise, Sadness, Disgust and Anger if it is not.
  • Asks a Wisdom Saving Throw only of creatures that aren't your allies. Allies never roll.
  • Needs a mind to work on: creatures with Intelligence 4 or lower can't be targeted.
  • Lands as a plain emotion if its reaction isn't unlocked yet. The old mood stays until the new emotion fades and replaces it.

Feelings

A feeling lasts 2 turns and leaves no mood behind. It does the same thing whoever carries it, so some feelings are gifts for your allies and others are traps for your enemies. The order of the two Surges doesn't matter: Joy on Accepting and Trust on Serene both braid Love.

Catharsis

Pour an emotion into its opposite and the threads snap at once. An enemy takes 2d8 Psychic damage; an ally (or you) regains 2d8 hit points. Every mood on the creature is spent. Catharsis is the Heartweaver's only real burst of damage, and it lands twice as hard on a creature already feeling Contempt.

Aspect of Empathy

Temperance awakens through mixing. Every reaction you cause during a fight counts towards your next tier: each feeling you braid, each overwhelming emotion and each Catharsis counts as 1, on allies and enemies alike, and every Catharsis caused by The Two Cups counts separately. Each goal you reach awakens one more tier, and everything lasts until combat ends. Your count shows on the Awakening Progress status.

Tier I · Attunement

Level 3 · cause 3 reactions

  • Catharsis deals or restores an additional 1d8.
  • Moods you create last 2 turns longer.
  • You can cast The Two Cups.

Tier II · Harmony

Level 7 · cause 5 reactions

  • Once per turn, when you braid a feeling, the nearest other creature within 6m gains the mood of one of that feeling's two emotions.

Tier III · Aspect of Empathy

Level 12 · cause 7 reactions

  • Your Wisdom increases by 3.
  • Your Weave Surges touch one more target.
  • Every reaction you cause on an ally also grants it temporary hit points.

Pouring for the Aspect

Harmony feeds itself: each feeling you braid leaves a fresh mood on the creature next to it, ready for your next Surge. At Tier III, a Surge cast with a level 6 slot touches 7 creatures, so a single action can braid, overwhelm and snap across half the battlefield.

Conditions & Terms

Weave Surge
The eight level 1 spells that fill a creature with one emotion: Joy, Trust, Fear, Surprise, Sadness, Disgust, Anger and Anticipation.
Emotion
The peak a Weave Surge creates, shown as Emotion: Joy, Emotion: Fear and so on. Lasts 1 turn. A creature feels one emotion at a time; a new one replaces the old.
Mood
The milder echo an emotion leaves when it fades on its own, shown as Mood: Serene and so on. Lasts 5 turns. A creature carries one mood at a time. A reaction spends it.
Reaction
What happens when a Weave Surge touches a creature that carries a mood: an overwhelming emotion, a feeling or Catharsis, decided by distance. Not the reaction you spend in combat.
Distance
The number of steps around the wheel between two emotions, from 0 (the same emotion) to 4 (opposites).
Opposites
Joy and Sadness, Trust and Disgust, Fear and Anger, Surprise and Anticipation.
Feeling
Two emotions braided into one, shown as Feeling: Love and so on. Lasts 2 turns and leaves no mood. A creature can carry several different feelings at once.
Overwhelming emotion
An emotion surged onto its own mood: Ecstasy, Admiration, Terror, Amazement, Grief, Loathing, Rage, Vigilance. Lasts 1 turn, then the mood returns for 5 turns.
Catharsis
Opposing emotions, snapped at once. An enemy takes 2d8 Psychic damage, an ally regains 2d8 hit points, and every mood is spent.
Disapproved
Shaken by an ally's Disapproval. Has Disadvantage on Attack Rolls.
Incapacitated
Can't move or take actions, bonus actions or reactions.
Stunned
Can't move or take actions or reactions. Automatically fails Strength and Dexterity Saving Throws, and attacks against it have Advantage.
Prone
