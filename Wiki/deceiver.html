The Deceiver

We keep the veil between what is and what seems, and the Weave lets us stand on either side of it.

A Deceiver

Mystra wove the Deceiver from the veil between what is and what seems, a thread that no one watching can follow. They scatter Doubles that shatter when struck, sow Doubt until enemies can't trust their own eyes, and bind them with Sigils and chains. The Deceiver fights from behind a crowd of illusions of themselves. Every Double that falls, every Sigil that bursts and every whisper that lands adds Doubt to an enemy's mind, until it breaks into delusion. Pick this destiny if you want to control a fight, fool your enemies and end it with one well-placed burst.

The Card

The second card of the Major Arcana shows a woman seated on a stone throne between two pillars, one black and one white. Behind her hangs a veil, and what lies beyond it is never shown. A scroll rests half-hidden in her lap and a crescent moon sleeps at her feet. She does not act, she does not speak. She knows, and she keeps what she knows to herself.

The High Priestess is the card of secrets and intuition: the truth that sits just past what the eye can see, and the quiet certainty of someone who has already looked. Her two pillars are the two sides of every question, what is and what seems. She stands between them, at the threshold, and belongs to both.

For the faithful of the Weave, she is Mystra as the Lady of Mysteries, the goddess who keeps the secrets of magic behind her own veil. The Deceiver is her chosen keeper. Where other destinies change the world, the Deceiver changes what the world believes. Your Doubles are what seems, you are what is, and no one watching can say which is which.

You read minds, wear any face you choose and hide among copies of yourself. You always know more than your enemies do.

Your enemies live the reversed card. They gain Doubt, fall to Paranoia and end the fight Deluded, unable to trust their own eyes.

Playing a Deceiver

The Deceiver is a controller who fights with illusions and finishes with bursts of Force and Psychic damage. A typical fight opens with Mirror Image to put Doubles on the field and raise your Armour Class, and Sigil of Malice on the most dangerous enemy. Your Doubles then take their own turns: they walk into enemy lines and Shatter, or, from level 5, cast their own copies of your spells to detonate your Sigils. Each of these adds Doubt, and every third stack leaves an enemy Deluded. Doubt also weakens Wisdom Saving Throws, so the more an enemy doubts, the more surely Veiled Prison and Marionette take hold.

At a Glance

  • Role: controller and burst caster who fights with illusions.
  • Strengths: control that grows as Doubt piles up; bodies on the field that raise your Armour Class, soak attacks and burst; strong single-target damage from Sigils, Mimic and your Doubles' spells; Telepathy, Faceless and Friends outside combat.
  • Weaknesses: no healing and only light armour; Doubles fall to any hit or area spell; damage needs setup; several control spells share Concentration.
  • Tip: a Double is never wasted. If an enemy destroys it, that enemy gains Doubt. If it Shatters first, every enemy around it does.

The Veil

Everything the Deceiver does feeds one engine with two halves. On the field stand your Doubles, illusions of you that fight, distract and burst. In your enemies' minds grows Doubt, which weakens them and, at its peak, leaves them Deluded. Your Sigil links the two: a mark that you and your Doubles detonate for extra damage and more Doubt.

Doubles

A Double is an illusion of you with 1 hit point and Armour Class 12. It wears your face and your clothes, takes its own turn in the initiative order and is yours to control. On its turn it has 9m of movement and one action, but no bonus action, no reaction and no weapon attacks: it acts only through its own spells, Dash and Disengage. It can't be healed, and it can't be Charmed, Possessed or Dominated, driven mad or made to flee.

For each Double you have, you gain +1 Armour Class (shown as The Veil (1) to The Veil (5) on your portrait). An enemy that destroys one of your Doubles gains Doubt: it believed the lie.

Shatter or be shattered

A Double counts for something however it ends. Destroyed by an enemy, it gives that enemy 1 Doubt. Shattered on its own turn, it gives 1 Doubt to every enemy within 3m. Only Dismiss, your limit and the end of combat take a Double away for nothing.

Doubt

Doubt is the condition your illusions leave behind: an enemy no longer sure what is real. It stacks up to three times on each creature and lasts 3 turns, refreshed every time a new stack lands.

  • Each stack reduces the creature's Wisdom Saving Throws by 1, and its Attack Rolls against you and your Doubles by 1.
  • The third stack leaves the creature Deluded for 1 turn: it acts at random and may lose its turn. At the start of each turn it can end the effect with a Wisdom Saving Throw against your spell save DC. Its Doubt then starts again from zero. A creature that can't be confused is unaffected.
  • At 2 or more stacks, a creature has Disadvantage on its Saving Throws against Veiled Prison and Marionette.

The Sigil

Sigil of Malice marks its target with your Sigil, and so does a Double's Mirror: Sigil of Malice. The mark waits until the creature takes damage from one of your Arcane spells, or one of your Doubles' spells, or is bound by your Ethereal Chains. Then it detonates: the creature takes extra Psychic damage and gains 1 Doubt. The spell that lays the Sigil doesn't detonate it, and a creature carries only one Sigil at a time.

Aspect of Doubt

In every fight, the Deceiver can awaken the Aspect of Doubt: the High Priestess behind her veil, seen at last. What counts is every stack of Doubt you apply, from any source. Stacks sown by your Doubles count as yours, and so does the stack that turns an enemy Deluded. Your progress shows on your portrait as Awakening Progress.

The goals add up over a single fight: 4 stacks for the first tier, 7 in total for the second, 10 in total for the third. Each tier also needs the character level that unlocks it (3, 7 and 12). Tiers stack, and everything they grant lasts until combat ends, when the count starts again from zero.

Tier I · Veiled

Level 3 · apply 4 stacks of Doubt

  • Enemies with Doubt have Disadvantage on Attack Rolls against you and your Doubles.
  • You can cast The Veil Lifts once this combat.

Tier II · Two Pillars

Level 7 · apply 7 stacks of Doubt

  • Once per turn, when an enemy becomes Deluded, the nearest unmarked enemy within 6m of it gains your Sigil.

Tier III · Aspect of Doubt

Level 12 · apply 10 stacks of Doubt

  • Your Charisma increases by 3.
  • Your Arcane spells add your Charisma Modifier to their damage.
  • You can use Ready: Which One? until combat ends.
