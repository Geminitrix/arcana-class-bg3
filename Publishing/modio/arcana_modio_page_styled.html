ARCANA

One class. Four destinies. Twenty levels.

✦  ✦  ✦

"The living bridge between mortal soul and divine aspect, weaving the raw threads of the cosmos into a chosen path across the fabric of reality."

Arcana is not a reskin and not a subclass pack. It is a complete class built from scratch — its own hit die, its own resource, its own progression table, its own spell list, its own icons, its own VFX, and four subclasses that each play like a different class entirely.

You pick your path at Level 1, and every level after that is written by hand. Nothing is copy-pasted from a vanilla class and left to rot: over 560 hand-written strings, over 130 custom spells and abilities, and full scaling all the way to Level 20.

WHY ARCANA IS DIFFERENT

  • Subclass from Level 1. No waiting until Level 3. Your fantasy starts on the character creation screen.
  • Arcane Cards. A single unified resource that replaces spell slots. No level-1-slot bookkeeping, no "I have three 2nd-levels left." Just cards.
  • Every subclass has its own chassis. Different primary ability, different saving throws, different armour and weapon proficiencies, different sound set, different starting outfit.
  • Expertise for everyone. Every Arcana chooses 4 skills and 2 Expertise at Level 1, regardless of path.
  • All 15 in-game languages. Not machine-dumped — checked line by line against Larian's own terminology.
  • Level 20 ready. Every damage die, heal, and unarmed strike has a hand-authored value for levels 1 through 20.

 

ARCANE CARDS

"Drawn from the primordial threads of The Weave, Arcane Cards has a mystical echo that fuels your gifts, adapting its power to the Arcana's chosen path."

Arcana does not use spell slots. It draws Arcane Cards.

  • You start with 8 cards at Level 1 and gain +2 every single level — 30 by Level 12, and it keeps climbing to Level 20.
  • Cards refresh on a Long Rest, and Arcane Recovery (Level 2) buys some back mid-day.
  • Cheap abilities cost one card. Your Level 11 and 12 capstones cost a fistful. You decide when to burn the deck.
  • Custom UI icon and resource panel entry — it looks native, because it was built to.

 

THE ARCANA CORE

Whatever path you take, the Arcana is a seer first. Divination is the thread every destiny is woven from, and it shows up as genuine out-of-combat power — the kind that changes how you play the game, not just how you win fights.

  • Level 1 — Arcane Hand, Comprehend Languages
  • Level 2 — Portent (replace a roll with a foreseen one), Detect Supernatural, Arcane Recovery
  • Level 4 — Expert Divination, Arcane Lock, Arcane Unlock
  • Level 6 — Dispel Magic
  • Level 8 — Third Eye — see through magical darkness and see Invisible creatures
  • Level 10 — Third Eye: Projection — detach your sight and scout ahead as an invisible eye
  • Level 12 — Arcane Gate — two linked portals, anywhere

The Deceiver doesn't beat the room — it rewrites who was in it. You mark targets with a Sigil that makes every following Arcane spell hit harder, then take enemies off the board one mind at a time. Telepathy from Level 1 means you are reading the table before anyone else has picked up their cards.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Is Arcana compatible with Level 20 mods?
Yes — properly. This isn't "it doesn't crash." Every damage die, heal, unarmed strike and scaling value in the class has a hand-written entry for all 20 levels. The class receives its designed progression through Level 12, and its numbers keep growing correctly to Level 20.

Do I have to wait until Level 3 to pick a subclass?
No. You choose your path in character creation, and each one starts with its own outfit, proficiencies, saving throws and abilities.

Does it use spell slots?
No. Arcana uses Arcane Cards, a single pooled resource that refreshes on Long Rest and can be partly recovered with Arcane Recovery.

Do the subclasses have unique Dialogue Tags?
Yes. Deceiver reacts as a Bard, Starchild as a Druid, Unbound as a Monk, and Eternal as a Paladin — chosen to match each identity for roleplay immersion.

Can I multiclass into Arcana?
Yes. It behaves like any other class in the multiclass screen.

Is it safe to add to an existing save?
Adding it mid-playthrough is fine. As with any class mod, removing it from a save where a character is using it is not — back up first.
