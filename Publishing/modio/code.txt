<p><strong>Arcana</strong> is not a reskin and not a subclass pack. It is a complete class built from scratch &mdash; its own hit die, its own resource, its own progression table, its own spell list, and four subclasses that each play like a different class entirely.</p>
<p>You pick your path at <strong>Level 1</strong>, and every level after that is written by hand. Nothing is copy-pasted from a vanilla class and left to rot: over <strong>560 hand-written strings</strong>, over <strong>130 custom spells and abilities</strong>, and a full progression of new features all the way to Level 20.</p>

<p style="color: #c579d9; font-size: 22px; font-weight: bold;"><strong>WHY ARCANA IS DIFFERENT</strong></p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Subclass from Level 1.</strong> No waiting until Level 3. Your fantasy starts on the character creation screen.</li>
<li><strong>Arcane Cards.</strong> A single unified resource that replaces spell slots. No level-1-slot bookkeeping, no "I have three 2nd-levels left."</li>
<li><strong>Every subclass has its own chassis.</strong> Different primary ability, different saving throws, different armour and weapon proficiencies, different sound set, different starting outfit.</li>
<li><strong>Expertise for everyone.</strong> Every Arcana chooses 4 skills and 2 Expertise at Level 1, regardless of path &mdash; because the fantasy of the class is being a specialist in something tied to the destiny you picked.</li>
<li><strong>All 15 in-game languages.</strong> Not machine-dumped &mdash; checked line by line against Larian's own terminology.</li>
<li><strong>Level 20 ready.</strong> Real content the whole way &mdash; empowered subclass abilities from Level 13, a tarot-named capstone at Level 20, and every damage die and heal hand-authored for all 20 levels.</li>
</ul>

<p style="color: #c579d9; font-size: 22px; font-weight: bold;"><strong>ARCANE CARDS</strong></p>
<blockquote>
<p>"Drawn from the primordial threads of The Weave, Arcane Cards has a mystical echo that fuels your gifts, adapting its power to the Arcana's chosen path."</p>
</blockquote>
<p>Arcana does not use spell slots. It draws <strong>Arcane Cards</strong>.</p>
<ul>
<li>You start with <strong>8 cards at Level 1</strong> and gain <strong>+2 every single level</strong> &mdash; 30 by Level 12, and it keeps climbing to Level 20.</li>
<li>Cards refresh on a <strong>Long Rest</strong>, and <strong>Arcane Recovery</strong> (Level 2) buys some back mid-day.</li>
<li>Cheap abilities cost one card, others cost more. You decide when to burn the deck.</li>
<li>A dedicated resource panel entry with its own custom icon (made by me), so Arcane Cards show up right alongside spell slots and every other resource.</li>
</ul>
<p>&nbsp;</p>

<p style="color: #c579d9; font-size: 22px; font-weight: bold;"><strong>LEVELS 13 TO 20 &mdash; ARCANE SHARDS</strong></p>
<p>Past Level 12, Arcana keeps writing new pages. In combat, casting an Arcane spell now generates <strong>Arcane Shards</strong> &mdash; a resource that only exists in battle, and fuels a new tier of empowered "EX" versions of your signature abilities.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Level 13</strong> &mdash; <strong>Arcane Shards</strong>: casting an Arcane spell in combat generates Shards equal to the spell's level.</li>
<li><strong>Level 14</strong> &mdash; <strong>Arcane Overflow</strong>: casting a spell recovers 1 Arcane Card.</li>
<li><strong>Level 16</strong> &mdash; <strong>Arcane Mastery</strong>: once per Long Rest, your next spell costs 0 Arcane Cards.</li>
<li><strong>Level 18</strong> &mdash; <strong>Arcane Veil</strong>: Resistance to all magical damage.</li>
<li><strong>Level 20</strong> &mdash; <strong>Arcane Avatar</strong>: +3 to Spell Attack Rolls, Spell Save DC and Intelligence, plus Advantage on Concentration saves.</li>
</ul>
<p>Every subclass also gets its own Level 13, 15, 17 and 19 upgrade &mdash; each one a renamed, reworked version of a signature ability, paid for with Arcane Shards instead of Arcane Cards &mdash; plus a Level 20 capstone named after its tarot card. See your path below.</p>

<p style="color: #c579d9; font-size: 22px; font-weight: bold;"><strong>FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS</strong></p>
<p><strong>Is it necessary to play past Level 12 for the base Arcana experience?</strong><br>No. Everything through Level 12 is the complete, core experience of the mod. From Level 13 onward, what you gain are exclusive, empowered versions of abilities you already received earlier &mdash; a bonus layer for long campaigns, not a requirement.</p>
<p><strong>Is Arcana compatible with Level 20 mods?</strong><br><strong>Yes &mdash; properly, and there's real content to back it up.</strong> Every damage die, heal, unarmed strike (for The Unbound) and scaling value in the class has a hand-written entry for all 20 levels, and from Level 13 onward you gain new Arcana-wide passives plus an empowered version of your subclass's signature ability. You gain feats at 4th, 8th and 12th level by default, or install one of the optional Feat Every Level / Feat Every 2 Levels add-ons to change the pace.</p>
<p><strong>Do I have to wait until Level 3 to pick a subclass?</strong><br>No. You choose your path in <strong>character creation</strong>, and each one starts with its own outfit, proficiencies, saving throws and abilities.</p>
<p><strong>Does it use spell slots?</strong><br>No. Arcana uses <strong>Arcane Cards</strong>, a single pooled resource that refreshes on Long Rest and can be partly recovered with Arcane Recovery.</p>
<p><strong>Do the subclasses have unique Dialogue Tags?</strong><br><strong>Yes.</strong> Deceiver reacts as a Bard, Starchild as a Druid, Unbound as a Monk, and Eternal as a Paladin &mdash; chosen to match each identity for roleplay immersion.</p>
<p><strong>Can I multiclass into Arcana?</strong><br>Yes, you can multiclass into it. It just isn't designed for that &mdash; Arcana was built as a complete class with its own beginning, middle and endgame. That said, you're free to build however you like.</p>
<p><strong>Is it safe to add to an existing save?</strong><br>Adding it mid-playthrough is fine. As with any class mod, <strong>removing</strong> it from a save where a character is using it is not &mdash; back up first.</p>
