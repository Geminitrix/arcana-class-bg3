|---|---|
| **The Wheel** / **on the Wheel** | The Kingmaker's mechanic; a creature carrying one of the four positions |

| **King · Regent** | The ally wearing the crown while Reigning; other Reigning allies |
| **Crossing** | Damage to an enemy that enters Fallen while an ally Reigns within 9 m |
| **Hubris** | Status of an enemy that reigned; its next Crossing is amplified |

| **King's Hand, Lord Commander, Master of Healing, Master of Laws, Master of Secrets, Master of Coin, Master of Ships** | The seven offices |
