# Arcana — The Heartweaver (XIV · Temperance · Aspect of Empathy)

Design approved in broad strokes on 2026-10-06 (name, arcanum, the distance rule, progression and
Awakening). Individual effects and numbers are still drafts until the balance pass. The chassis is
deliberately left for later (§12).

The sixth Arcana subclass. Every other Arcana embodies **one slice** of the wheel of emotions — Love
(Empress), Hope (Star), Curiosity (Fool) and Sorrow (Death) are all points on Plutchik's wheel — while the
Heartweaver **sees the whole wheel** and braids one emotion into another with the threads of the Weave.

It is also the first subclass built on the **Depth Model** (§2), the bar the other four will be raised to.

Everything here is Stats and Osiris, so it runs in the **console edition** (no Script Extender). The PC
edition adds nothing to it beyond the deck layer every card already has.

## 2. The Depth Model

A subclass is "complete" when it meets these eight criteria. The Heartweaver is designed from them; the
other four will be audited against the same list (§13).

| # | Criterion | In the Heartweaver |
|---|---|---|
| M1 | **An engine of its own**: a state only this subclass creates and reads. | Surge → mood → reaction |
| M2 | **The engine grows with level** — levels unlock *new rules*, not only bigger cards. | Primary (1) → secondary (5) → intense (7) → tertiary (9) |
| M3 | **Cards in three roles**: generators (create state), converters (change state), payoffs (spend state). | Surges · Turn the Wheel, Emotional Contagion · Catharsis, The Two Cups |
| M4 | **Allies and enemies** both take part in the engine. | Every reaction exists on both sides |
| M5 | **The Awakening is the engine's climax**: its goal counts uses of the engine. | Counts reactions caused |
| M6 | **Readable**: one rule explained once, visible state, its name in the combat log. | The distance rule on one passive; Empathic Sight shows the state |
| M7 | **Lore tied to a real source** (tarot, psychology, myth) plus Mystra and the Weave. | Plutchik + Temperance ("Art" in the Thoth deck) + threads of the Weave |
| M8 | **No Script Extender** in the engine (both editions) and **generated by a script** once the scale outgrows hand editing. | Stats + Osiris; `gen_emotion_alchemy.pl` |

## 4. The engine: Emotional Alchemy

### 4.1 The wheel

Clockwise: **Joy, Trust, Fear, Surprise, Sadness, Disgust, Anger, Anticipation**.
Opposites: Joy↔Sadness · Trust↔Disgust · Fear↔Anger · Surprise↔Anticipation.

| Emotion (peak) | Mood (mild ring) | Intense (inner ring) | Side without a mood |
|---|---|---|---|
| Joy | Serene | Ecstasy | ally |
| Trust | Accepting | Admiration | ally |
| Fear | Apprehensive | Terror | enemy |
| Surprise | Distracted | Amazement | enemy |
| Sadness | Pensive | Grief | enemy |
| Disgust | Bored | Loathing | enemy |
| Anger | Annoyed | Rage | enemy |
| Anticipation | Interested | Vigilance | ally |

### 4.2 The distance rule

A Surge cast on a creature that already carries a mood **reacts** with it, by the distance between the two
emotions on the wheel:

| Distance | Reaction | Plutchik | Unlocked at | Lasts |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | **Intensify** → intense ring | intensity | level 7 (*Overwhelm*) | 1 turn, then back to the mood |
| 1 | **Primary feeling** | primary dyad | level 1 | 2 turns |
| 2 | **Secondary feeling** | secondary dyad | level 5 (*Complex Feelings*) | 2 turns |
| 3 | **Tertiary feeling** | tertiary dyad | level 9 (*Mixed Feelings*) | 2 turns |
| 4 | **Catharsis** | opposites | level 1 | instant |

- The reaction **spends** the mood.
- A reaction still locked (passive not learnt): the Surge lands as a plain peak.
- No mood: the Surge lands as a plain peak; when it expires it leaves its mood (replacing the previous one).
- **Stacking:** peaks and intense emotions share `StackId EMOTION` - a creature feels **one emotion** at a time, so one mood comes out of it. Moods share `MOOD` - **one mood** at a time, so each Surge produces at most one reaction. Every **feeling** has its own StackId (its status id), so different feelings coexist.

### 4.3 Targets and saves

- **Any** Surge can target **any** creature that carries a mood.
- Without a mood: Joy, Trust and Anticipation only on allies (or yourself); the other five only on non-allies.
- A **Wisdom** save only when the target is a non-ally. Allies never roll.
- All require `IntelligenceGreaterThan(4)` (an emotion needs a mind).

### 4.4 How many states

**48 emotional states** (an even number: 8 peaks + 8 moods + 8 intense + 24 feelings) **plus Catharsis**,
which is an instant event rather than a state that lasts, for 49 statuses at the core. The generator also
writes 4 helper statuses (`FEELING_DISAPPROVED`, two `_TECH` locks, `HEARTWEAVER_DEBUG`).

## 9. Technical pattern

### 9.1 The generator

`82_Automation/stats/gen_emotion_alchemy.pl` (working copy in `82_Automation/stats/`) reads **one table** (wheel
order, names, boosts and text of every state) and writes patches for `stats_add.pl`:
- the 8 `Target_Arcana_Card_Spell_Emotion*` spells with the reaction matrix in `SpellSuccess`;
- every status, each in the file of its type;
- the English strings.

Changing a number means editing the table and running it again. Then: `gen_upcast_variants.pl`,
`sort_stats.pl --apply`, `check_cards.pl`.

### 9.2 Generated `SpellSuccess` (example: Fear)

The "no reaction" case comes **first**, before any functor spends a mood. A creature only carries one mood,
so at most one of the other `IF`s is true.

```
IF(not (HasStatus('MOOD_APPREHENSIVE') and HasPassive('Arcana_Passive_Overwhelm',context.Source))
   and not HasStatus('MOOD_ACCEPTING') and not HasStatus('MOOD_DISTRACTED')
   and not (HasStatus('MOOD_SERENE') and HasPassive('Arcana_Passive_ComplexFeelings',context.Source))
   and ... and not HasStatus('MOOD_ANNOYED')):ApplyStatus(EMOTION_FEAR,100,1);
IF(HasStatus('MOOD_APPREHENSIVE') and HasPassive('Arcana_Passive_Overwhelm',context.Source)):ApplyStatus(EMOTION_TERROR,100,1);
IF(HasStatus('MOOD_ACCEPTING')):ApplyStatus(FEELING_SUBMISSION,100,2);
IF(HasStatus('MOOD_DISTRACTED')):ApplyStatus(FEELING_AWE,100,2);
... (secondary and tertiary with their HasPassive)
IF(HasStatus('MOOD_ANNOYED') and not Ally() and not Self()):DealDamage(2d8,Psychic,Magical);
IF(HasStatus('MOOD_ANNOYED') and (Ally() or Self())):RegainHitPoints(2d8);
IF(HasStatus('MOOD_ANNOYED')):ApplyStatus(CATHARSIS,100,0)
```

(Line breaks for reading only; in the file it is one line.)

### 9.3 Statuses

- Peaks and intense: `OnRemoveFunctors` = `IF(RemoveCause(StatusRemoveCause.TimeOut)):ApplyStatus(MOOD_*,100,5)`
  — Terror, when it passes, goes back to Apprehensive.
- Feelings and intense: `OnApplyFunctors` = `RemoveStatus` of the mood they spend.
- Catharsis: damage or healing is decided in the Surge's `SpellSuccess` (by `Ally()`); the `CATHARSIS`
  status only clears the moods and shows the name in the log.
- A status with a vanilla parent declares `StatusType` before `using`, and keeps the parent's
  `OnApplyFunctors` (e.g. `BreakConcentration()`) when it adds its own.
- Joy stops being KNOCKED_DOWN (leaves `Status_KNOCKED_DOWN.txt`, becomes BOOST).

### 9.4 Ids and what exists today

| What | Ids |
|---|---|
| Spells | `Target_Arcana_Card_Spell_Emotion{Joy,Trust,Fear,Surprise,Sadness,Disgust,Anger,Anticipation}` |
| Passives | `Arcana_Passive_{EmotionalAlchemy,EmpathicSight,ComplexFeelings,Overwhelm,MixedFeelings}` (+ hidden `FeelingRemorse`, `FeelingMorbidness`) |
| Renamed | `EmotionFury`→`EmotionAnger`, `EMOTION_FURY`→`EMOTION_ANGER`, the 6 current `MOOD_*` |
| Removed | `Target_Arcana_Card_Spell_Feeling*` (8), the old `EmotionSurprise` placeholder, any leftover `EMOTION_FEAR_DAMAGE`/`_TECH` |

Checked on 2026-10-06: nothing outside `Spell_Target.txt` references these spells (no list, CardDB or Lua),
so the renames are free. A card with no `CardDB` entry costs its own level (`D.CostOf`).

## 10. Text (English, Larian voice, Mystra and the Weave)

A spell Description is an imperative + an image of a thread of the Weave + the visible consequence, no
numbers. Rules go in ExtraDescription. A status is a participle phrase + the mechanics after `<br><br>`.
British English, spaced hyphen, Title Case only for rules terms (style memory).

| Piece | Mould |
|---|---|
| Spell | `Weave Surge: <Emotion>` — "<Verb> a thread of <emotion> in a creature's Weave, <consequence>." |
| Surge ExtraDescription | "When the surge fades, it leaves the target <Mood>. If the target already carries a mood, the threads react instead - see <Emotional Alchemy>." |
| Peak | `Emotion: <Emotion>` — "Gripped by a thread of <emotion> in the Weave." |
| Mood | `Mood: <Mood>` — "<Participle> by the last echo of <emotion> in the Weave." |
| Intense | `Emotion: <Intense>` — "<Emotion> pulled so tight in the Weave that it overwhelms." |
| Feeling | `Feeling: <Feeling>` — "Threads of <emotion A> and <emotion B>, braided into one." |
| Catharsis | "Opposing threads of the Weave snapped at once, releasing everything they held." |
| Emotional Alchemy passive | explains the rule: same thread → overwhelm · neighbouring → braid · opposite → snap |

The 8 Surge descriptions and every status text live in the generator's table.
