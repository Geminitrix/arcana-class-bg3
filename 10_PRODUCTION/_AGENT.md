# 10 · Production & Agile Management — agent rules

- **Owner:** Executive Producer / Scrum Master (active)
- **Writes here:** the owner only. Others ask through `HANDOFF_<from>_to_<to>.md` placed in this folder and mark their ticket `blocked_by`.
- **Read before working:** `/CLAUDE.md`, `/AGENTS.md`, `05_LIVE_CONTEXT/05.00_READ_FIRST.md`, this file, then only the files the ticket lists by ID.

## Conventions

- Tickets: `ARC-####_Short_Title.md`, YAML header (id, type, status, owner, implementation, inputs, outputs, blocked_by).
- BACKLOG.md and SPRINT_BOARD.md are generated by `82_Automation/update_board` — never edited by hand.
- Sprint length: 1 week. Daily log written at the end of every work session.

## Quality checklist

- [ ] Ticket meets DoR (00.03) before entering a sprint.
- [ ] Board regenerated after ticket changes.
